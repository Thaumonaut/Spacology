# Cross-run progression and end game in tactics/strategy and hub-building roguelites (Into the Breach, FTL, Darkest Dungeon 1 & 2, Loop Hero, Against the Storm, Dicey Dungeons)

Research date: 2026-10-08. Most primary facts below date from 2012–2025. I found no 2026 changes to these systems; treat everything as "as of last reported update". Several primary pages (Epic Games Store news, Fandom/wiki.gg wikis, Hooded Horse wiki, GamesMarkt interview) returned 401/402/403/404 errors, so some details rest on search snippets and secondary sources. Those cases are flagged.

## Q1. How do achievement-gated unlocks (Into the Breach / FTL) work as retention without power?

### Takeaway
Subset Games ties new *options* (squads, ship layouts) to achievements that each ask you to play a particular way. In Into the Breach each squad comes with three squad-specific achievements, and each achievement pays 1 coin toward the next squad. The unlocks add variety, not power. They also act as an "extended tutorial" that teaches combos and synergies, so players come back to try a new set of rules rather than to grow stronger.

### Cited Findings
- **Into the Breach (2018) coin loop.** Extra squads are unlocked by completing achievements, and every completed achievement earns 1 coin. The Secret Squad costs 25 coins — [GamingOnLinux, AE launch, Jul 2022](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2022/07/into-the-breach-advanced-edition-update-is-out-now)
- **Advanced Edition (free update, July 2022)** added 5 new squads and 15 new squad achievements, and **lowered squad coin costs** to make room for the extra squads — [GamingOnLinux](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2022/07/into-the-breach-advanced-edition-update-is-out-now). PC Gamer: "our favorite game of 2018 got even better in 2022" — [PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/into-the-breach-advanced-edition-2022)
- **Unfair difficulty (AE)** is "for veterans", with "more situations where damage is inevitable", so the player more often has to choose between taking grid damage, sacrificing a pilot, or failing an objective. It is the top of the Easy/Normal/Hard/Unfair ladder — [GamingOnLinux](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2022/07/into-the-breach-advanced-edition-update-is-out-now)
- **Achievements as teaching.** Reviewers note that working through the achievements means you "pretty much learn how to play the game effectively, including how to set up certain kinds of combos". "Most of the achievements serve as an extended tutorial." Each new squad has "its own unique synergies, with three unique achievements to provide a little guidance on how you should use them", like FTL's variant ships — [gamecritics.com review](https://gamecritics.com/dan-lipscomb/into-the-breach-review/), [Digital Trends review](https://digitaltrends.com/gaming/into-the-breach-review) (the search snippets do not make clear which of these two sources contains which quote)
- One stated design challenge was "teaching players to get comfortable with sacrificing their mechs and pilots" — [Cliqist interview with Justin Ma, Mar 2018](https://cliqist.com/2018/03/06/into-the-breach-building-a-better-mech-with-subset-games-justin-ma/)
- **GDC 2019, "'Into the Breach' Design Postmortem" (Matthew Davis).** Covers four years of cut content and iteration: what to cut, how to "steal" design elements, "how to decide on correct difficulty", and "how much RNG to use" — [GDC Vault](https://gdcvault.com/play/1025772/-Into-the-Breach-Design); [80.lv preview](https://80.lv/articles/gdc-2019-an-inside-look-at-into-the-breach). The FTL postmortem is also on video: [Game Developer: "Designing without a pitch – the FTL postmortem"](https://gamedeveloper.com/audio/video-designing-without-a-pitch---the-i-ftl-i-postmortem); see also [How Subset made the jump from FTL to Into the Breach](https://gamasutra.com:443/business/how-subset-games-made-the-jump-from-i-ftl-i-to-i-into-the-breach-i-)
- **FTL (2012; Advanced Edition 2014) ship unlocks.** Only the Kestrel is available at first. Reaching sector 5 with the Kestrel unlocks the Engi Cruiser. For other ships, **Layout A** comes from a ship-specific quest or from beating the Flagship with the previous ship. **Layout B** needs 2 of that ship's 3 ship-specific achievements. **Layout C** (AE) needs sector 8 with Layout B. The Lanius unlocks after 4 ships. The Crystal Cruiser unlocks via a quest, or by beating the Flagship with Layouts A and B of every ship — [Pocket Gamer unlock guide](https://www.pocketgamer.com/ftl-faster-than-light/how-to-unlock-every-ship), [FTL Wiki – Ship](https://ftl.fandom.com/wiki/Ship)
- **FTL Hard mode** came with the Advanced Edition and has "lower scrap rewards and other tweaks" — [FTL Wiki – Advanced Edition](https://ftl.fandom.com/wiki/FTL:_Advanced_Edition)
- **Dicey Dungeons (Terry Cavanagh, Aug 2019)** has six characters (Warrior, Thief, Robot, Jester, Inventor, Witch) — [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/861540/Dicey_Dungeons/). Its cross-run structure is a set of authored **episodes** per character, and each episode is a rule twist. The free **Reunion** update (7 July 2022, alongside the mobile launch) added six new episodes and "around 5 hours of new content". For example, the Thief episode is about stealing enemy equipment, and the Warrior episode uses a Yacht-style category-dice system — [Nintendo Life](https://nintendolife.com/news/2022/06/terry-cavanaghs-dicey-dungeons-rolls-out-huge-new-free-dlc-reunion-soon), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dicey_Dungeons). A 2019 **Halloween Special** offered three time-limited episodes (Warrior, Inventor, Witch), each "a significant change to the basic game", available for one week — [itch.io devlog](https://terrycavanagh.itch.io/dicey-dungeons/devlog/106966/halloween-special)
- Dicey Dungeons Metacritic: PC 80, Switch 88, **iOS 98**. Reviewers called it "a good introduction into roguelike deck-building games" — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dicey_Dungeons)

### Inferences
- In the Subset model, retention comes from **curiosity about a new rule-set plus a stated goal**, not from compounding stats. The achievement tells you what to look for, and the squad or ship gives you new tools to look with. A run does not get easier because of an unlock. It gets different, and the "hidden" lesson is made explicit.
- The coin sink (1 coin per achievement, spent on any squad) gives players a little agency over unlock order. Layout B's "2 of 3 achievements" rule gives slack, so one very hard achievement does not block content.
- Dicey Dungeons is an even purer version: authored "remix" episodes (rule mutators) work like a curriculum of reading new rules. This is directly analogous to a reading/conditions game.
- **For the asker (premium iPad, reading game, no power grind):** a "Long Census" entry could be unlocked by an achievement-style *demonstration of understanding* (for example, "win a fight where condition X triggered three times"). The entry then unlocks a new crew or rule-set variant. That is the ITB pattern of unlocks as teaching. A Dicey-style episode list per crew (authored rule twists) is the second lever. Neither adds power.

## Q2. How do hub/base-building metas function, and when do they become grind?

### Takeaway
Hubs like the DD1 Hamlet, Loop Hero's camp, the DD2 Altar and Against the Storm's Smoldering City turn run rewards into persistent upgrades and unlocks. They become "grind" when costs scale faster than one interesting run pays out, when content and power are mixed together, or when the currency keeps flowing after there is nothing left to buy. Developers have repeatedly fixed this by splitting content from power, by cutting costs (DD1 Radiant), or by building a separate long-form mode (DD2 Kingdoms).

### Cited Findings
- **Darkest Dungeon 1 Hamlet.** Heirlooms upgrade buildings. The **Radiant** mode exists specifically to reduce grind: "buildings require less heirlooms to upgrade", service costs are lower, heroes level faster, and leftover provisions return more gold — [DD wiki.gg – Game Modes](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Game_Modes); Radiant launched Feb 2017 — [The Outer Haven](https://www.theouterhaven.net/2017/02/darkest-dungeon-radiant-update-now-live-pc/). Radiant also lifts the "Never Again" rule (heroes who have completed a Darkest Dungeon run can go back in) — [DD wiki.gg](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Game_Modes)
- **Loop Hero (Four Quarters, Mar 2021) camp.** Resources from expeditions build and upgrade camp structures, which unlock cards, classes (Warrior, Rogue, Necromancer) and mechanics — [BlueStacks guide](https://www.bluestacks.com/blog/game-guides/loop-hero/lh-beginners-guide-en.html). Retention rules: you keep 100% of resources if you retreat at the camp tile, **60%** if you retreat elsewhere, and **30%** on death — [Twinfinite](https://twinfinite.net/guides/loop-hero-return-to-camp-how/). Resources are collected as shards that combine (for example, 12 sticks become 1 lumber) — [Goonhammer](https://www.goonhammer.com/loop-hero-a-boardgame-rpg-hybrid-with-an-excellent-gameplay-flow)
- **Loop Hero grind criticism.** "becomes, quite intentionally, a bit of a grind." Later chapters "deteriorate into more of a grind for resources". Mid-to-late structures need "a ton of rare materials". Players have asked for an "unlock all" button. The endgame was called "repetitive and grind-heavy" — [Goonhammer](https://www.goonhammer.com/loop-hero-a-boardgame-rpg-hybrid-with-an-excellent-gameplay-flow), [RPGFan](https://rpgfan.com/review/loop-hero), [Indie Game Reviewer](https://indiegamereviewer.com/loop-hero-review-keeps-goin-and-goin-and-goin/), [Nintendo Life](https://nintendolife.com/reviews/switch-eshop/loop-hero). (Snippet-level attribution: the summary does not pin each quote to a specific outlet.)
- Loop Hero's first major patch (1.1, June 2021) added in-expedition saves plus **expedition and flight speed sliders**. These are quality-of-life tools that shorten grind time without changing costs. Sales were above 800k at the time — [TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/news/loop-heros-update-brings-in-expedition-saves-expedition-and-flight-speed), [GamingOnLinux](https://gamingonlinux.com/2021/06/loop-hero-adds-saving-during-a-run-and-more-speed-options-in-the-11-update), [Automaton (JP)](https://automaton-media.com/articles/newsjp/20210616-165656/)
- **Against the Storm (Eremite Games; EA 2022, 1.0 Dec 2023).** The Smoldering City is described as "an avatar of progress that you invest in and develop between runs, leaving a permanent mark on the World Map" — [Eremite devlog 4](https://eremitegames.com/devlog-4-meta-progression-new-biome-and-more/)
- **The Against the Storm "Progression Update" (Update 11, v0.28.1R, 16 Mar 2022)** started the "Phase of the Citadel". Eremite found two core problems: some buildings felt worse because of RNG, and "the progression tree conflated two concepts: new content and power-ups." The fix was to **separate content from power**. Unlocks (buildings, perks, traders, species) now come automatically with player level. The Citadel Upgrade Tree holds only permanent enhancements, which went from 47 to 65 upgrades, 19 of them new. The level cap rose from 12 to 15. Eremite refused requests to ban blueprints, keeping the principle of "making the best with the hand you're dealt" and adding limited cornerstone rerolls instead — [Eremite – Progression Update](https://eremitegames.com/progression-update/)
- Player view: "The meta upgrades are nice, but not super crucial unless you play at the punishing end of the prestige levels" — [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1336490/discussions/0/3547176090245096910) (secondary, via search summary)

### Inferences
- Grind shows up when: (a) the hub's price curve rises steeply in late chapters (Loop Hero); (b) the hub sells power, which pushes players into "farm, then attempt"; or (c) the currency keeps dropping with nothing left to buy (DD2 candles, Q4).
- Eremite's "content arrives by level, power sits in a separate tree" split is a useful structure. Even better for a no-power-grind game is to drop the power tree entirely and keep only content and codex.
- **For the asker:** a Long Census "catalogue as hub" can grow visibly, like DD2's Altar restoring the Kingdom's appearance or the Smoldering City changing the map. It can be fed by *what you learned/observed* (new species, conditions or answers recorded) rather than a farmable currency, which closes off the grind failure mode. If any currency exists, it should stop or turn into something else once the catalogue is complete.

### Gaps
- No HowLongToBeat data or Steam achievement percentages were retrieved for hours-to-complete-camp (Loop Hero) or Hamlet max-out (DD1).
- No Four Quarters developer statement responding to the grind criticism was found.

## Q3. How are prestige/difficulty ladders structured and received?

### Takeaway
These games use either a few flat tiers (ITB Easy→Unfair, FTL Easy/Normal/Hard) or permadeath-style campaign constraints (DD1 Stygian/Bloodmoon week and death limits). Against the Storm stands out with a 20-step Prestige ladder stacked on top of Impossible. Short ladders are well received as optional mastery. Long ladders give hardcore players a goal for hundreds of hours, but players report that the top end narrows the choices and turns into repetition.

### Cited Findings
- **ITB:** Unfair (AE, 2022) sits above Hard. More "inevitable damage" situations force trade-offs — [GamingOnLinux](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2022/07/into-the-breach-advanced-edition-update-is-out-now)
- **FTL:** Hard mode was added in AE (2014), with lower scrap and other tweaks — [FTL Wiki](https://ftl.fandom.com/wiki/FTL:_Advanced_Edition)
- **DD1 Stygian** (formerly "New Game+", which used to require beating the game first) has a week limit and a hero-death limit that delete the campaign if exceeded. It also has +20% enemy HP, +3% enemy crit, faster light drain, and all options locked at maximum difficulty. The failure conditions are removed after you beat the final boss — [DD wiki.gg – Game Modes](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Game_Modes). **Bloodmoon** (Crimson Court DLC) uses Stygian mechanics with different thresholds.
  - **Conflict:** wiki.gg (via fetch) gives Stygian as 85 weeks / 12 deaths and Bloodmoon as 100 weeks / 16 deaths. A search-snippet summary of the same wiki described Bloodmoon as 86 weeks / 12 deaths. Treat the exact numbers as unverified. The likely relationship is that Bloodmoon is *more lenient* on limits than Stygian, but it is not certain.
- **Against the Storm Prestige:** an "optional difficulty ladder of 18 levels above the Impossible difficulty for hardcore players" in Early Access — [Steam discussion / Eremite via search](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1336490/discussions/0/3487502671133250024). At 1.0 it is commonly cited as **P1–P20**, which matches the asker's framing; the number grew over development. Glade event parameters also scale with difficulty — [Eremite – Progression Update](https://eremitegames.com/progression-update/)
- **Against the Storm reception at high Prestige** (Steam forum sentiment): near P20 you need "100+ years" (in-game cycles) of Citadel effort. "The score multiplier is meaningless and citadel resources don't matter much after a while". The number of viable options "reduced dramatically" at top prestige. Players complain of doing the same openings every map, with most mechanics ignored in favour of the few that keep you alive. "P15+ takes a toll" — [Steam discussions](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1336490/discussions/0/3875969774718716509), [another thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1336490/discussions/0/4038104133467985518) (aggregated from multiple threads by the search tool; individual quote-to-thread mapping is not verified). The game still holds roughly 94% positive Steam reviews — [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/With-94-positive-reviews-this-dark-fantasy-city-builder-is-70-off-on-Steam.1376143.0.html)
- **Meta power vs Prestige:** the Citadel tree gives permanent power (65 upgrades as of 2022) — [Eremite](https://eremitegames.com/progression-update/). Players say these upgrades only become crucial at the punishing top of the Prestige ladder — [Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1336490/discussions/0/3547176090245096910). So the meta power is effectively *budgeted* against the ladder: power growth makes up for difficulty growth.

### Inferences
- Ladders without power (ITB, FTL) are pure mastery: the challenge is skill only and finite. Ladders paired with a power tree (Against the Storm) blur the question of whether a win reflects skill or accumulated upgrades. That is a reason a no-power-meta game should keep its ladder small, legible and fully skill-based.
- Long ladders produce a "solved openings" monotony at the top, because the stacked modifiers remove options. A reading game that adds *new conditions* at each tier, rather than simply harsher numbers, avoids this. That is closer to ITB's Unfair ("forced trade-off situations") than to stat inflation.
- DD1 shows a different axis: **campaign constraints** (time and death budgets) rather than stat inflation. These fit a long-form "survey voyage" framing well, for example a census completed within N voyages.

### Gaps
- I could not access the full Against the Storm P1–P20 modifier list (the Hooded Horse wiki returned 403).
- No Eremite GDC talk or official statement on balancing meta-upgrades against Prestige was found. No Steam global achievement rates for ITB Unfair, FTL Hard or Against the Storm P20 were retrieved.

## Q4. What did DD2's meta-progression controversy teach? (plus end-game assessment across titles)

### Takeaway
DD2's Early Access progression was a linear "Hope"/XP track with no choices. Red Hook replaced it with the choice-driven **Altar of Hope** (Candles of Hope, Sep 2022) and reset all progress. Even after 1.0 (8 May 2023), players objected that candles kept dropping after the Altar was complete, leaving runs with no sense of progress. Red Hook's real answer was **Kingdoms** (27 Jan 2025): a free, parallel long-form mode built around DD1-style persistent management of inns and heroes. Community response to it was "overwhelmingly positive". The lessons are that a meta needs real choices, that it must not outlast its purpose, and that some players come back for a *persistent world to steward*, not for an unlock tree.

### Cited Findings
- **Altar of Hope update** (announced and on the experimental branch on 27 Sep 2022; main branch about 1–2 weeks later) **reset player progression** and granted 20 Candles of Hope as compensation — [NME](https://www.nme.com/news/darkest-dungeon-2-update-introduces-a-new-progression-system-3318046), [GameBanshee](https://gamebanshee.com/xyqwk)
- Red Hook's creative director: "the old 'Hope'/XP system didn't afford players any real choice in terms of how to progress their metagame between runs" — [GameBanshee](https://gamebanshee.com/xyqwk), and the Epic Store editorial "[Darkest Dungeon 2's metagame reboot was expected and necessary](https://store.epicgames.com/news/darkest-dungeon-2s-metagame-reboot-was-expected-and-necessary)" (403 on fetch; quote taken via search snippets)
- **Mechanics.** Candles are spent across four categories: General Upgrades, Heroes, Items and Memories. There is a risk/reward cash-out: ending a run at an Inn keeps your candles, while a mid-region wipe costs candles. Reaching the Mountain keeps all candles even if you lose. "Memories" persist only while a hero survives, and heroes who reach the final boss keep theirs. **The Kingdom's appearance is visually restored as you invest hope** — [NME](https://www.nme.com/news/darkest-dungeon-2-update-introduces-a-new-progression-system-3318046), [DualShockers](https://www.dualshockers.com/darkest-dungeon-2-altar-of-hope-update-introduces-new-progression-system/), [GameBanshee](https://gamebanshee.com/xycku)
- 1.0 launch date: 8 May 2023 — [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2023/02/darkest-dungeon-ii-launches-may-8)
- **Post-launch complaint.** "Remove candles permanently after fulfilling the altar of hope. Maybe replace it with relics." A reply called this "a unanimous sentiment on this forum since I joined it last month." Posted 26 Jan 2024 — [Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1940340/discussions/0/4138312860566110288). A related claim, that unlocking everything "only takes 40 hours or less" after which runs give "no satisfaction or sense of progress", appears in DD2 Steam discussions or review aggregates (search snippet; exact source not confirmed) — [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1940340/discussions/0/3830918083267510134), [Vaporlens DD2 summary](https://vaporlens.app/app/1940340/darkest_dungeon_ii). Other complaints describe "repetitive grind": "every run the same enemies, the same regions, the same moves" — [Vaporlens](https://vaporlens.app/app/1940340/darkest_dungeon_ii)
- **Kingdoms mode.** Announced in 2023 for 2024 — [Game Developer press release](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/press-release/darkest-dungeon-ii-introduces-kingdoms-a-new-game-mode-coming-in-2024). It launched free on 27 Jan 2025 alongside the Inhuman Bondage DLC. Players race to defeat a monstrous threat before it destroys the Kingdom's network of Inns, gathering resources to upgrade heroes and Inns through upgrade trees. It runs "as a parallel experience to the original Confessions game mode": you travel by stagecoach, upgrade inns, complete quests and fight new factions (Coven, Beastmen, Crimson Courtiers) — [Turn Based Lovers](https://turnbasedlovers.com/news/darkest-dungeon-ii-to-launch-new-free-game-mode-kingdoms-and-dlc-inhuman-bondage-on-january-2025/), [GamesPress](https://gamespress.com/Darkest-Dungeon-IIs-Brand-New-Free-Game-Mode-Kingdoms-Red-Hook-Studios), [Darkest Dungeon news](https://www.darkestdungeon.com/news/kingdoms-game-mode-inhuman-bondage-dlc-available-now) (404 on fetch)
- **Kingdoms reception.** The community response was "overwhelmingly positive". Players praised its strategic depth and its blend of DD1 and DD2 elements as a "fresh and engaging long-form experience". Automaton (Jan 2025) framed it as a large free mode that boosted popularity, implemented as the developer's bid to "recover" ("挽回") — [Darkest Dungeon "Kingdoms progress report"](https://darkestdungeon.com/news/kingdoms-progress-report) (via search snippet; 404 on fetch), [Automaton (JP)](https://automaton-media.com/articles/newsjp/darkest-dungeon-kingdoms-20250128-326648/). A Feb 2025 GamesMarkt interview with Red Hook on long-term support and the Behaviour takeover is paywalled — [GamesMarkt](https://www.gamesmarket.global/business/interview-red-hook-on-darkest-dungeon-ii-long-time-support-strategies-and-the-takeover-by-behaviour-5f43de2b90243011c9c0af897e7a23f4)

### End-game summary per title (where it runs out and what long-term players do)
- **Into the Breach:** content runs out once all squads (plus Secret Squad) and achievements are done. Long-term players chase Hard/Unfair clears with each squad, random/custom squads, and community challenge runs. AE added squads, Unfair and new enemies to restart this loop for free — [GamingOnLinux](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2022/07/into-the-breach-advanced-edition-update-is-out-now). (Hours to unlock everything: no sourced figure.)
- **FTL:** the end game is completing all ship layouts (A/B/C), driven by achievements, then Hard-mode wins with each — [Pocket Gamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/ftl-faster-than-light/how-to-unlock-every-ship)
- **DD1:** the Hamlet maxes out, then Stygian/Bloodmoon campaigns with permadeath limits provide the end game — [DD wiki.gg](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Game_Modes)
- **DD2:** the Altar completes (players say in about 40 hours or less, unconfirmed), candles become meaningless, and Kingdoms is the long-form answer — sources above.
- **Loop Hero:** the camp grind in later chapters is the main criticism, and the endgame is "repetitive and grind-heavy" — [RPGFan](https://rpgfan.com/review/loop-hero)
- **Against the Storm:** the Prestige climb to P20 plus Citadel completion. At the top, choices narrow and openings become routine — Steam threads above.
- **Dicey Dungeons:** finite authored episodes. Reunion (2022) added about 5 hours of new episodes, after which the game is "done" — [Nintendo Life](https://nintendolife.com/news/2022/06/terry-cavanaghs-dicey-dungeons-rolls-out-huge-new-free-dlc-reunion-soon)

### Inferences
- DD2 lessons:
  1. A linear, choice-free meta feels like a toll gate.
  2. Resetting progress during Early Access was accepted ("expected and necessary"), but only with compensation and an explanation.
  3. A currency that keeps flowing after the store is empty actively harms the feeling of progress.
  4. Players who wanted DD1's sense of *stewardship* (a persistent hamlet and roster) were not served by a lighter unlock tree, and a separate long-form mode served them better than tuning the tree.
- Kingdoms' success suggests the "why return" motive for this audience is a **persistent place and cast that you care for**, not a stat ladder. That supports the asker's codex/hub idea: a Long Census that fills in, reshapes the ship or map visually (like the Altar restoring the Kingdom), and records crew history.
- **Design implications for a premium no-monetisation iPad reading roguelite:**
  - (a) Achievement-gated variety unlocks that teach a condition-reading skill (ITB).
  - (b) Authored rule-twist episodes per crew (Dicey).
  - (c) A small, skill-only difficulty ladder where higher tiers add new condition types rather than stat inflation (ITB Unfair, not Against the Storm P15+).
  - (d) A codex/hub that grows through knowledge, has a clear "complete" state, and stops issuing currency afterwards (avoiding DD2's candles problem).
  - (e) Optionally, a long-form campaign mode with time and attrition budgets (DD1 Stygian, DD2 Kingdoms) for players who want stewardship.
  - Loop Hero's speed sliders are a reminder that touch players value respecting their time.
