# Cross-run meta-progression and end game in action roguelites (Hades / Hades II, Dead Cells, Risk of Rain 2, Vampire Survivors, Rogue Legacy 1/2)

Research date: October 2026. Facts are current to the cited source dates. Version-sensitive items are flagged. Several primary wiki pages (hades.fandom.com, vampire-survivors.fandom.com) returned HTTP 402 to the fetch tool, so some system details come from secondary guides. Anything I could not source is in the Gaps sections and marked as unverified.

## Which games use permanent power, and how do core and casual players receive it?

### Takeaway
All five games give some permanent power between runs. The amount ranges widely. Rogue Legacy makes the manor the main source of progress, which has drawn criticism since 2015. Hades and Dead Cells use it as a cushion for the early game, and Hades' Heat system can switch the Mirror off. Vampire Survivors makes PowerUps refundable and toggleable. Risk of Rain 2 gives almost no stat power: you unlock items and survivors (more options), not bigger numbers. Casual players and accessibility advocates value the cushion. Core players criticise it for blurring self-assessment and as a "forced crutch". In response, developers have moved toward explicit, penalty-free assist options (Hades God Mode, Rogue Legacy 2 House Rules, Dead Cells Assist Mode) rather than relying on grind as the hidden easy mode.

### Cited Findings

**Rogue Legacy (2013), the main example of permanent power**
- In the original, the Architect lets you re-enter the same castle layout but takes 70% of the gold you earn. Reviewers called the game "a bit of a grindfest": players took the Spelunker trait and rushed chests for gold. You also had to hand over unspent gold to the gatekeeper before re-entering, so a run that earned 800 gold toward a 1,200-gold upgrade was "all for naught". — [Niche Gamer review / search summary](https://nichegamer.com/reviews/rogue-legacy-review-roguelike-platforming-at-its-finest/); [Riptide Lab "A Skill Grind"](https://riptidelab.com/?p=832)
- Josh Bycer (Game Developer, 16 June 2015) on Rogue Legacy and Shattered Planet: "Because your progression is locked to the Meta-game, it means that you are tethered to it to have any chance at winning." He also wrote that "you literally can't play far into these titles on your basic setup and the Meta-game becomes a forced crutch". He recommends meta-game as "icing on the cake" and cites The Binding of Isaac, where unlocks add options while the base game stays fully playable. — [The Pitfalls of Meta Game Design, gamedeveloper.com](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-pitfalls-of-meta-game-design)

**Rogue Legacy 2 (Cellar Door Games, 1.0 in April 2022)**
- Cellar Door acknowledged that many players wrongly believed grinding was mandatory in the original, and designed the sequel to fix this. The team worried that building only for skilled RL1 players would exclude others. They also made everything faster because players had become used to Dead Cells and Enter the Gungeon. — [Gameblog.fr interview (French)](https://www.gameblog.fr/jeu-video/ed/news/rogue-legacy-2-metroidvania-difficulte-philosophie-une-interview-devoile-de-nouvelles-infos-85591) (via search summary)
- House Rules let players set Enemy Health and Enemy Damage anywhere from 50% to 200%, turn on flight, and turn off contact damage, all "with no penalty at all". — [The Workprint review](https://www.theworkprint.com/rogue-legacy-2-review-bigger-bolder-better/123); [Seasoned Gaming review](https://seasonedgaming.com/2022/04/28/review-rogue-legacy-2-a-breath-of-fresh-heir)
- RL2's Architect (unlocked through the Screw Distillery node) locks the castle layout in exchange for 20% of gold found, down from RL1's 70%. — [search summary citing Rogue Legacy 2 guides](https://www.godisageek.com/reviews/rogue-legacy-2-review)
- RL2 keeps the manor upgrade tree and adds Heirlooms, permanent traversal abilities that persist between runs, metroidvania style. — [The Workprint](https://www.theworkprint.com/rogue-legacy-2-review-bigger-bolder-better/123)
- Nintendo Indie World interview with designer Teddy Lee about lessons from RL1 (full text not retrieved). — [Nintendo Indie World interview](https://www.nintendo.com/whatsnew/indie-world-interview-rogue-legacy-2-game-designer-teddy-lee-talks-about-the-newly-available-heir/)

**Hades (Supergiant, 1.0 in September 2020)**
- The Mirror of Night gives permanent talents. The Pact of Punishment's Routine Inspection condition disables 3 Mirror talents per rank, from the bottom up, across 4 ranks, so up to 12 talents can be turned off. Players can therefore undo their meta power in exchange for Heat. — [TheGamer / search summary](https://www.thegamer.com/hades-heat-pact-punishment-trivia/); [Prima Games Pact guide](https://primagames.com/gaming/hades-guide-pact-of-punishment-modifiers-heat)
- God Mode starts at 20% damage resistance and adds 2% per death, up to 80%. It can be turned on at any time during a run. Kasavin: "Our big focus from the start with Hades was how to take the sting of failure and reduce that as much as possible, knowing that it inherently feels bad to die in a game." He also said: "With more damage resistance, you can afford to make a few more mistakes, and more importantly, you have a bit more time to study those encounters." — [Inverse interview](https://www.inverse.com/gaming/hades-god-mode-interview); [Can I Play That?](https://caniplaythat.com/2021/08/11/hades-god-mode-explained-by-supergiant-games)
- Kasavin: "God Mode reinforces our belief that the way to approach difficulty settings may need to be proprietary to the game. It's not a one size fits all solution." Also: "it's not easy to tune a video game", and on roguelikes: "The part that's interesting about roguelikes is that it's different every time you play... To experience that, they've got to kill you." — [Can I Play That?](https://caniplaythat.com/2021/08/11/hades-god-mode-explained-by-supergiant-games)
- Kasavin: "There's nothing more frustrating in games where you're really engaged with the story, but then you just hit a difficulty wall, and you just want to see how the story ends." Supergiant builds systems so story progress isn't blocked by skill. — [Game Developer / GDC Podcast ep. 16](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/roguelikes-and-narrative-design-with-i-hades-i-creative-director-greg-kasavin)

**Dead Cells (Motion Twin / Evil Empire, 1.0 in August 2018)**
- Cells are collected from enemies and spent on permanent upgrades and unlocks between runs. Everything else is lost on death. — [Wikipedia, Dead Cells](https://en.wikipedia.com/wiki/Dead_Cells); [80.lv interview](https://80.lv/articles/interview-with-the-developers-of-dead-cells)
- The developers' stated aim is to "empower the player, right from the get go", with each challenge "tough but not insurmountable". Motion Twin also said that moveset mastery is needed to progress. — [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/interview-with-the-developers-of-dead-cells); [PlayStation Blog, 2018](https://blog.eu.playstation.com/2018/01/25/gorgeous-rogue-lite-action-platformer-dead-cells-is-battling-onto-ps4-this-year/)
- Sébastien Bénard (CNC interview, title): "La plus grosse difficulté était de ne pas lasser le joueur" ("the biggest difficulty was not boring the player"). — [CNC.fr](https://www.cnc.fr/jeu-video/actualites/sebastien-benard-dead-cells---la-plus-grosse-difficulte-etait-de-ne-pas-lasser-le-joueur_905050)
- The Breaking Barriers update (June 2022, with AbleGamers) added Assist Mode: lives from 1 to infinite, auto-hit with the primary weapon, sliders for enemy damage and health, map reveal, a wider parry window, and slower, weaker traps. Achievements stay enabled, but Assist Mode scores are hidden from the Daily Challenge leaderboard. — [GamingBolt](https://gamingbolt.com/dead-cells-breaking-barriers-update-is-live-adds-assist-mode-and-accessibility-options); [Twinfinite](https://twinfinite.net/2022/06/dead-cells-breaking-barriers-update-gives-more-accessibility-options/)
- Version 3.0 removed stat bonuses from certain legendary items, and Aspects were added as an alternative progression system. Using an Aspect prevents earning the next Boss Stem Cell. — [Dead Cells wiki, Boss Stem Cells](https://deadcells.wiki.gg/wiki/Boss_Stem_Cells)

**Vampire Survivors (poncle, 1.0 in October 2022)**
- There are 27 PowerUps, counting Seal variants. Prices rise with each purchase: Price = InitialPrice × (1 + Bought) + ⌊20 × 1.1^TotalBought⌋. A "Refund PowerUps" button (added in patch 0.2.5) returns all gold. Maxed PowerUps (except Reroll, Skip, Banish, and Seal) can be toggled off without a refund. — [Vampire Survivors wiki, PowerUps](https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/PowerUps)
- The toggle for maxed PowerUps arrived in v1.2 (15 December 2022). — [Prima Games v1.2 patch notes](https://vip-develop.primagames.com/?p=88874); [Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/how-to-reset-progress-in-vampire-survivors/)
- Curse is itself a PowerUp (#15). It raises enemy difficulty in exchange for more gold, which makes difficulty a purchasable stat. — [VS wiki](https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/PowerUps)
- Peter Howell (The Conversation, 13 April 2023): Luca Galante drew on "his previous experience in the gambling industry". Runs ending before the 30-minute mark trigger a "near miss effect". "No run ever feels wasted as players feel a sense of increasing mastery and progression, even if they only gain a little gold." Free respec encourages build experimentation. — [The Conversation](https://theconversation.com/vampire-survivors-how-developers-used-gambling-psychology-to-create-a-bafta-winning-game-203613)

**Risk of Rain 2 (Hopoo, 1.0 in August 2020; Gearbox-owned since 2022)**
- Eclipse gives no unlocks or rewards: "it is strictly a challenge mode to test players' skills". Its only rewards are visual markers on the character-select screen. — [RoR2 wiki, Eclipse](https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/wiki/Eclipse)

**Player sentiment**
- Lemmy/Fediverse thread "Meta progression in roguelites was fun for a while, but it's starting to feel unrewarding". Points made: Rogue Legacy made losing nearly welcome, but meta progression "blurs self-evaluation". You lose and can't tell whether you need more unlocks or more understanding. It also lets players "continue playing badly and still win eventually", so they don't build fundamentals. Some prefer the FTL model, with no power gain between runs, which was standard "before the genre morphed around the time of Rogue Legacy". — [Lemmy thread](https://lonestarpiefed.duckdns.org/post/24798)

### Inferences
- Over 2013–2022 the trend has been to separate "make it easier" from "make me grind". RL1 used grind as the de facto difficulty setting. RL2, Hades, and Dead Cells added explicit, penalty-free sliders or modes. That suggests these developers came to see grind as a poor accessibility tool, and that a no-grind game needs an honest assist option instead.
- Hades' Routine Inspection, Vampire Survivors' toggles and refunds, and Dead Cells' Aspects blocking Boss Cell progress are all ways to subtract meta power. Core players clearly want "pure" runs even in grind-based games, which supports a self-contained-run design.
- Risk of Rain 2 is the closest analogue to a no-power-grind design. Its persistence is almost entirely horizontal (items, survivors, skins, logs), and its ladder (Eclipse) has no rewards.
- Lesson for a premium iPad auto-battler: casual players get two things from permanent power. Failure feels productive, and they have a guaranteed path to the credits. Both can be provided without stat grind, through unlocks that add options (Isaac/RoR2 style), narrative or codex progress on every run, and an explicit assist mode in the style of God Mode or House Rules.

## How does narrative across runs (Hades) function as retention, and when does it end?

### Takeaway
Hades makes death the story's delivery mechanism. The protagonist is immortal, characters remember previous encounters, and there are about 22,000 voiced lines, so almost every death produces new dialogue. The main story ("The Family Secret") ends after the first escape plus further escapes. The epilogue needs ten escapes, and only 8.4% of Steam players reach it. Much of the playerbase stops at the first escape or the credits, so narrative carries players through the mid-game but doesn't hold them indefinitely. Hades II (1.0 on 25 September 2025) uses the same model. Its epilogue achievement sits at 9.4%.

### Cited Findings
- Kasavin designed "a roguelike with narrative continuity" in which "every time you run into a boss, they remember, keeping a tally of who won this time, who won last time". The thing that normally persists in roguelikes is "your knowledge of the mechanics". He made it the story as well, with an immortal protagonist who keeps his memories. — [Game Developer / GDC Podcast](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/roguelikes-and-narrative-design-with-i-hades-i-creative-director-greg-kasavin); [GDC news](https://gdconf.com/news/roguelikes-and-narrative-design-hades-creative-director-greg-kasavin-gdc-podcast-ep-16)
- Kasavin also argues for including the "dumb stuff", pointless but special details, because developers can't predict what will resonate, and more of it raises the odds that something does. — [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/roguelikes-and-narrative-design-with-i-hades-i-creative-director-greg-kasavin)
- GDC 2021 talk "Breathing Life into Greek Myth" (Kasavin and Darren Korb): a studio of fewer than 20 people shipped more than 22,000 lines of voiced dialogue. Early Access "turned out to be a huge boon for the narrative", even though Early Access is traditionally a poor fit for story games. — [GDC Vault](https://gdcvault.com/play/1026975/Breathing-Life-into-Greek-Myth); [GDC news](https://gdconf.com/news/dive-dialogue-hades-gdc-2021)

### Inferences
- Across the Hades funnel, retention falls about 50% between the first escape (46.6%) and the main quest (23.3%), and again from the main quest to the epilogue (8.4%). Narrative pulls players past the first win, but only a minority follow it to the end. It works as a strong mid-game hook more than as an end-game sustainer.
- The story "ends" in two stages: the credits, after the main quest, and the epilogue, which needs repeated escapes and relationship progress. After that, players are left with Heat, bounties, Prophecies, and Aspects.
- Lesson for a self-contained-run auto-battler: narrative or codex progress that advances on every run, including losses, gives "failure feels productive" without any power growth. It is the cleanest substitute for stat meta-progression. Its cost is content volume (Hades had 22K lines), which is a real constraint for a small team.

## How do Heat, Boss Cells, and Eclipse-style ladders sustain the end game?

### Takeaway
All of these ladders are opt-in sets of modifiers that stack. They split into two kinds:
- **Ladders tied to rewards:** Hades' Heat bounties pay currency per weapon per Heat level. Dead Cells' Boss Cells unlock biomes, a seventh stage, and higher cell multipliers.
- **Pure-skill ladders:** Risk of Rain 2's Eclipse 1–8 gives only cosmetic markers. Rogue Legacy 2's Threads of Fate goes up to NG+100 with Burdens. Hades II's Fear and Testaments, and Vampire Survivors' Curse, Hyper, and Inverse, also scale difficulty.

Per-character or per-weapon tracking multiplies ladder length: a full ladder for each Hades weapon, each RoR2 survivor, each Hades II aspect. Completion rates show that only a small core climbs very far.

### Cited Findings

**Hades: Pact of Punishment / Heat**
- The Pact unlocks after the first escape. Heat measures difficulty, and each condition adds Heat. Bounties re-award Titan Blood (from the Furies), Diamonds (from the Bone Dragon), and Ambrosia (from Theseus and the Minotaur) for each new Heat level per weapon. They exist "to incentivize you to employ the Pact of Punishment." — [RPG Site](https://rpgsite.net/feature/10287-hades-pact-of-punishment-heat-modifiers-and-how-to-maximize-your-rewards); [Prima Games](https://primagames.com/gaming/hades-guide-pact-of-punishment-modifiers-heat)
- Skelly's hidden prizes unlock after escapes at 8, 16, and 32 Heat. 32 Heat is the community's standard target. — [DualShockers 32 Heat guide](https://www.dualshockers.com/hades-beating-pact-of-punishment-32-heat-run-guide/)

**Hades II: Oath of the Unseen / Fear**
- Maximum Fear is 67, and "only 3 players in the world" had beaten 67 Fear at the time of the source. Testament rewards start at 32 Fear, and each successive Testament needs more Fear. Documented community ceilings are 62–65 Fear across four aspects, with 67 reported on an unseeded run. — [Brokenbuilds Fear guide](https://brokenbuilds.gg/hades-ii/guides/fear); [Mobalytics](https://mobalytics.gg/hades-2/guides/oath-of-the-unseen) (version-dependent; numbers may change with patches)
- "Gifts of the Veil" achievements: 24.1% earned the first, 16.7% the second. — [Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/stats/1145350/achievements)

### Inferences
- Heat and Boss Cells use rewards to pull players up the ladder: the climb pays currency or unlocks new areas. Eclipse is pure prestige. RoR2's ladder still holds players because it is per-survivor (well over a dozen survivors × 8 levels) and runs vary a lot. A reward-free ladder can work if build variety is high.
- Per-unit ladders (per weapon or aspect in Hades, per survivor in RoR2) multiply end-game length without new content. For an auto-battler, per-faction or per-commander difficulty tiers are a cheap, grind-free way to extend play.
- Dead Cells and RL2 both chose to end live development (2025 and 2023) rather than keep adding. Motion Twin explicitly cited not wanting to dilute the game. For a premium game, a finite and complete game is a legitimate design.
- Boss Cells make difficulty and content unlocks the same thing: you only reach the Astrolab at 5 BC. That rewards the core but locks content away from most players (only 7.7% beat Dracula). Hades II's Testaments and Hades' bounties have the same issue on a smaller scale.

## What design critiques exist of each approach?

### Takeaway
The main critiques:
1. **Meta-progression as a crutch** (Rogue Legacy most of all, and Vampire Survivors' gold PowerUps). Permanent power blurs whether you improved and makes early runs feel like grinding.
2. **Grind and economy friction.** RL1's 70% Architect tax and gold that had to be spent or surrendered.
3. **Content locked behind difficulty** (Dead Cells' Astrolab at 5 BC).
4. **Engineered compulsion.** Vampire Survivors' gambling-derived reward loops.
5. **Live-ops quality risk.** RoR2's Seekers of the Storm.

The fixes developers adopted point the same way: explicit, penalty-free difficulty options, refundable or toggleable power, and ladders players opt into.

### Cited Findings
- **Crutch critique (2015, still widely cited):** In Rogue Legacy, meta progression is "the only real form of progression". It ties the player's chance of winning to the meta-game and makes it "a forced crutch". Meta should be "icing on the cake". — [Josh Bycer, Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-pitfalls-of-meta-game-design); [Game Wisdom, "When Meta Game Design Doesn't Work"](https://game-wisdom.com/?p=11175)
- **Self-evaluation critique (player sentiment, 2020s):** Meta progression "blurs self-evaluation" and lets players win while playing badly. Some players prefer FTL-style, power-free designs. — [Lemmy thread](https://lonestarpiefed.duckdns.org/post/24798)
- **Grind economy (RL1):** the Architect's 70% tax, gold surrendered at the gate, and chest-rushing with Spelunker. — [Niche Gamer](https://nichegamer.com/reviews/rogue-legacy-review-roguelike-platforming-at-its-finest/); [Riptide Lab](https://riptidelab.com/?p=832)
- **RL2 response:** House Rules with no penalty, a 20% Architect tax, and an acknowledgement that players wrongly felt grinding was mandatory. — [Gameblog.fr](https://www.gameblog.fr/jeu-video/ed/news/rogue-legacy-2-metroidvania-difficulte-philosophie-une-interview-devoile-de-nouvelles-infos-85591); [The Workprint](https://www.theworkprint.com/rogue-legacy-2-review-bigger-bolder-better/123)
- **Hades response:** God Mode exists as a respectful alternative to grind or a fixed easy mode. Kasavin warned it may not transfer to other games: difficulty design is "proprietary to the game". Routine Inspection lets experts remove Mirror power. — [Inverse](https://www.inverse.com/gaming/hades-god-mode-interview); [Can I Play That?](https://caniplaythat.com/2021/08/11/hades-god-mode-explained-by-supergiant-games); [Prima Games](https://primagames.com/gaming/hades-guide-pact-of-punishment-modifiers-heat)
- **Dead Cells response:** Assist Mode keeps achievements but hides scores from daily leaderboards. v3.0 removed stat bonuses from some legendaries. The studio ended development rather than overextend. — [GamingBolt](https://gamingbolt.com/dead-cells-breaking-barriers-update-is-live-adds-assist-mode-and-accessibility-options); [Dead Cells wiki](https://deadcells.wiki.gg/wiki/Boss_Stem_Cells); [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/evil-empire-has-officially-ended-support-for-dead-cells)
- **Vampire Survivors critique:** gambling-industry psychology (near-miss effect, layered rewards). Mitigations: free refunds, toggleable maxed PowerUps, and Curse as a voluntary difficulty purchase. — [The Conversation](https://theconversation.com/vampire-survivors-how-developers-used-gambling-psychology-to-create-a-bafta-winning-game-203613); [VS wiki](https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/PowerUps)
- **RoR2 critique:** Eclipse has no rewards, and some players see that as a strength. The bigger criticism concerns post-acquisition DLC quality and balance, not meta-progression. The original Hopoo developers voiced concerns about the Gearbox DLC. — [RoR2 wiki](https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/wiki/Eclipse); [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/risk-of-rain-2/dlc-gearbox); [IndieCator](https://indiecator.org/2024/08/31/the-risk-of-rain-2-seekers-of-the-storm-controversy/)
- **Hades narrative trade-off:** the story is the retention hook, but it needed a huge content budget (22K+ lines). Early Access was used to iterate on it. — [GDC Vault](https://gdcvault.com/play/1026975/Breathing-Life-into-Greek-Myth)

### Inferences (relevance to a premium, no-grind iPad auto-battler with self-contained runs)
- The critiques largely support a self-contained-run model. Each game added ways for players to remove meta power, which suggests core players value runs whose outcome reflects decisions alone.
- The risk of rejecting meta power is losing the casual safety net. The proven substitutes are:
  1. Horizontal unlocks: new units, factions, or commanders that add options rather than strength (RoR2, Isaac).
  2. A narrative or codex that advances on every run, including losses (Hades).
  3. An explicit, penalty-free assist mode that is transparent and not shamed (God Mode, House Rules, Dead Cells Assist Mode). Note Dead Cells' compromise: achievements stay on, leaderboards are separate.
  4. Opt-in difficulty ladders tracked per unit or faction (Heat, Eclipse) for the core.
- Completion data shows that "seeing everything" is a minority goal in every game. Only 8–9% reach the Hades or Hades II epilogue, 7.7% beat Dracula in Dead Cells, and 4.4% earn False Son Mastery in RoR2. Design the first credits for the majority (roughly 45–50% reach a first win in these games) and the ladder for the 5–20% core.
- Ending live development deliberately (Dead Cells, RL2) is reasonable for a premium game. A complete game that ends is a positive, not a failure of retention.
