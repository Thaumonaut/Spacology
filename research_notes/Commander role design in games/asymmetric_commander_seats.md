# Asymmetric Commander / Captain Seats in Multiplayer and Co-op Games

Scope note: researched October 2026. Most primary material on these games is old: BF2 is from 2005, BF2142 from 2006, BF4 and its tablet app from 2013-2015, NS2 from 2012-2016, Nuclear Dawn from 2011, the Savage series from 2003-2008, and the KTANE GDC talk from 2016. Squad's commander dates from 2019, Hell Let Loose (HLL) is ongoing, and Carrier Command 2 is from 2021 with console ports announced in August 2026. Evidence on commander shortage and toxicity is almost entirely anecdotal from forums and Steam threads. I found no developer usage statistics for any title. Empires (Source mod) returned no usable sources and is listed as a gap.

## What makes the commander seat rewarding for the people who love it (and what the seat actually is, per game)?

### Takeaway
People who love the seat value outsized impact through other people: one player's map view, spending and orders visibly shape the whole team's match. Recognition helps too ("a great commander could really set herself apart"), and the role fits players who prefer strategy to aim. In social co-op (Artemis, KTANE), the chaos of shouted orders and laughing at disasters is itself the reward.

### Cited Findings

**Natural Selection 2 (Unknown Worlds, 2012; RTS/FPS hybrid)**
- Lineage: in the original NS, marines carried out the orders of a player-commander who saw the battle from above and could distribute resources or start construction of upgrades and buildings. A PopMatters writer recalled that "a great commander could really set herself apart from others." — [Wikipedia: Natural Selection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_Selection_(video_game)); [PopMatters](https://www.popmatters.com/159485-natural-selection-2495844417.html)
- NS2 added an Alien commander as well. Charlie Cleveland said the reason was to balance the game for larger-scale matches. The alien commander spreads infestation by placing nodes. — [PopMatters](https://www.popmatters.com/159485-natural-selection-2495844417.html)
- Cleveland framed the core design tension as trading fun for RTS players against fun for FPS players: "We want and need to try new things but we need to appeal to our base too or we're sunk" (pre-release, circa 2010-2011). — [Hooked Gamers interview](https://www.hookedgamers.com/features/2011/01/07/charlie_cleveland_on_natural_selection_2.html)
- Unknown Worlds introduced separate commander skill ratings (Hiveskill 3.0: per-team and commander Hiveskill), which formally recognises commanding as a distinct skill. — [Unknown Worlds news](https://unknownworlds.com/en/news/introducing-hiveskill-3-0-per-team-and-commander-hiveskill-thank-you-moultano)
- A player defending the role says a good commander who communicates well makes the game much better, and that the commander is part of what makes NS2 unique among shooters. — [Steam: "I wish there was no commander role"](https://steamcommunity.com/app/4920/discussions/0/828925849512173303/)
- A patch circa 2015 capped how many structures and assets a commander can have deployed at once. This works as a resource/supply budget on the seat. — [Unknown Worlds forum guide](https://forums.unknownworlds.com/discussion/131205/natural-selection-2-a-complete-guide-updating-soon)

**Battlefield 2 (2005) / 2142 (2006)**
- Commander loop: one per team, working from an overhead map. Players apply for the post and the highest-ranked applicant is favoured. Assets were satellite scan (enemy positions shown to the commander only, who must relay them by voice or text), artillery (lands after several seconds and usually wipes out infantry in the blast), UAV (reveals an area, cannot be shot down in the air, but depends on a ground trailer near the main base that enemies can destroy with 2 C4 packs or about 30 tank shots, and a teammate must repair it or a supply drop must land nearby), and supply drops of ammo and health crates to squads. — [GameSpot BF2 hands-on (preview, 2005)](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/battlefield-2-hands-on-on-foot-and-commander-view/1100-6114811/); [Battlefield wiki: UAV (Commander Resource)](https://battlefield.fandom.com/wiki/UAV_(Commander_Resource)); [AnandTech "Commander mode explained"](https://forums.anandtech.com/threads/battlefield-2-strategies-commander-mode-explained.1621302/). A roughly 30-second scan recharge appears only in a Grokipedia summary. It is unverified. [Grokipedia](https://grokipedia.com/page/Battlefield_2)
- BF2142 kept the role mostly unchanged. It added "Move Titan" in place of Orbital Strike in Titan mode and kept the mutiny vote. — [Wikipedia: BF2142](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battlefield_2142)

**Battlefield 4 (2013) commander and tablet app**
- DICE's design intent: "more Commander vs. Commander gameplay (similar to what can be seen in RTS games)" and "We wanted to ensure the Commander is dedicated to supporting the whole team, not just themselves." The seat became full-time, with no switching to first-person as in BF2. — [PCGamesN (updated May 2014)](https://www.pcgamesn.com/battlefield/battlefield-4-commander-mode-gives-load-powers-player-hot-seat)
- Assets: UAV scans; EMP to disable the enemy commander's UAVs; cruise-missile warning; High Value Target (marks an enemy on a 6+ killstreak for 45 s, alerts both teams, gives a kill bonus, and was inspired by GTA's "wanted stars"); cruise missile strike, which requires the ground team to capture launch pads; AC-130 gunship that doubles as a mobile spawn; squad promotion, which pushes a squad's unlock bar; vehicle parachute drops; halved squad respawn timer; and supply crates that heal, rearm and allow class switching. — [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/battlefield/battlefield-4-commander-mode-gives-load-powers-player-hot-seat)
- Squad orders: the commander sets attack or defend orders, the squad leader relays them, and squads earn points for completing orders. — [Namu Wiki BF4/Commander](https://en.namu.wiki/w/%EB%B0%B0%ED%8B%80%ED%95%84%EB%93%9C%204/%EC%A7%80%ED%9C%98%EA%B4%80); [Dignitas field guide](https://dignitas.gg/articles/blogs/Battlefield/4209/battlefield-4-a-field-guide-part-1-whats-new)
- PC Gamer's review credited the mode with improving the game. It described the commander as watching a full-screen map, issuing attack and defend orders, and calling in assets that squads effectively earn like killstreaks. Tom's Guide listed it as a pro ("makes teamwork essential"). — [PC Gamer BF4 review](https://www.pcgamer.com/battlefield-4-pc-review/); [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/uk/us/battlefield-4-review,review-1942.html)
- Tablet app: iPad 2+ and iPad mini on iOS 6.1+, later Android. Players joined via Battlelog's "Commander" button. The app required owning BF4 and rank 10+. A later update added squad-member visibility, VOIP indicators and order positions. EA ended mobile Commander support on 28 April 2015. — [MP1st](https://mp1st.com/news/official-battlefield-4-tablet-commander-app-now-live-ios-later-android); [Battlefield.com: Commander app](https://www.battlefield.com/news/battlefield-4-commander-app-out-now-on-android-and-ios-1); [Battlefield.com: tweaks and improvements](https://www.battlefield.com/news/commander-app-tweaks-and-improvements); [PCGamesN app article](https://www.pcgamesn.com/battlefield/battlefield-4-commander-app-deployed-tablets-ios-first)

**Squad (Offworld Industries; commander added B17, 26 Nov 2019)**
- Launch framing: a leadership role focused on planning, keeping the team on task and bringing reinforcements, and keeping intel flowing between squads. Tools were a UAV to mark and scout, plus artillery and airstrikes. Conventional factions get 155mm artillery and CAS. Militia and insurgent factions get handheld drones and heavy mortars. The commander is chosen by a team vote. — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/squads-latest-update-introduces-commanders-uavs-and-artillery-strikes/); [Squad wiki release versions](https://squad.fandom.com/wiki/Release_Versions)
- Later factions add more call-ins, for example the AFU's SU-25 precision bomb strike, Bayraktar TB2 recon UAV and 152mm barrage in v10. — [Squad 10.0 patch notes](https://www.joinsquad.com/updates/squad-10-0-patch-notes)

**Hell Let Loose (Black Matter/Team17)**
- One commander per team. The commander directs squads by talking to unit leaders and has offensive and defensive abilities. Resources are Manpower, Munitions and Fuel, at 30 per minute base plus 10 per resource node, up to 3 nodes. Abilities include Reinforce (boosts capture in a sector), Encouraged, Final Stand, Dismantle Garrison, Airhead (an airdropped temporary spawn), supply drops, recon planes and bombing runs. Abilities cost resources and have cooldowns. Sources disagree on costs (for example Airhead is listed at 75 or 400 with a 10-minute cooldown), probably because of version differences. — [HLL Fandom: Commander](https://hellletloose.fandom.com/wiki/Commander); [The Loadout guide](https://theloadout.com/hell-let-loose/commander-guide); [Guildorder fan wiki](https://guildorder.com/games/hell_let_loose/wiki/commander-role-and-strategic-abilities)
- Most successful commanders spend most of the match on the map screen rather than in combat. That suits strategy-minded players. — [The Loadout](https://theloadout.com/hell-let-loose/commander-guide)
- In HLL: Vietnam, the commander also builds and maintains spawn infrastructure and directs squad leaders through the leadership voice channel. — [GameRant HLL Vietnam guide](https://gamerant.com/hell-let-loose-vietnam-commander-guide/)

**Savage: The Battle for Newerth (2003) / Savage 2 (2008)**
- The commander has a top-down RTS view, builds structures with team gold (mined from nodes or donated by action players), issues orders, draws on the minimap and casts spells on players. The commander can promote players to Officer, and officers can issue orders and give nearby allies healing or buffs. Loss of the command centre ends the match. — [Wikipedia: Savage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savage:_The_Battle_for_Newerth); [Wikipedia: Savage 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savage_2:_A_Tortured_Soul)

**Nuclear Dawn (InterWave, 26 Sep 2011)**
- One commander per team, picked at random. The commander leads from an overhead view, sets up infrastructure and spawn points, and researches tech and erects buildings that unlock new abilities for the other players' classes. — [Wikipedia: Nuclear Dawn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_Dawn)
- Steam store pitch: organise players into squads, assign tactical objectives, plan resource routes, and produce structures for forward deployment. Players report the commander can deploy turrets, supplies and reinforcements. — [Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/17710/Nuclear_Dawn/)

**Artemis Spaceship Bridge Simulator (Thom Robertson, solo indie dev)**
- The captain has no dedicated screen and no controls. They decide policy and strategy and give commands to the stations (helm, weapons, science, engineering, comms). Their information comes from the shared main viewer, an optional sector map, and what the crew reports. — [Wikipedia: Artemis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artemis:_Spaceship_Bridge_Simulator); [Goodreads blog](https://www.goodreads.com/author_blog_posts/3102459-gaming-artemis-spaceship-bridge-simulator); [VideoGameGeek](https://videogamegeek.com/videogame/125542/artemis-spaceship-bridge-simulator)
- Reviewers: Nerdist calls the captain the hardest job, coordinating all the information from the crew. Captains "curse a lot, watch their ship get kicked around... and then laugh with their friends as they hit the reset button." A Board Game Quest captain was "constantly moving between stations, trying to keep the crew functioning." — [Nerdist](https://nerdist.com/article/test-your-starship-command-skills-with-artemis-bridge-simulator/); [Board Game Quest](https://www.boardgamequest.com/artemis-spaceship-bridge-simulator-review/)
- Tom's Guide was critical. It found station gameplay "extremely simplistic" (following instructions and pushing the same buttons) and the game not fun without six imaginative players. — [Tom's Guide](https://tomsguide.com/us/review-artemis-bridge-simulator,review-1928.html)

**Carrier Command 2 (Geometa/MicroProse, 10 Aug 2021)**
- Up to 9 players crew one carrier in co-op or PvP. A 3D bridge has monitors per system (one for spotting targets, one for ordering supplies, and so on). Each unit can be controlled manually or automatically. Console ports were announced 24 Aug 2026. — [Wikipedia: Carrier Command 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrier_Command_2)
- In multiplayer, each player can take manual control of any drone instead of leaving it on AI orders. The game is real-time under pressure from an advancing enemy. Critics cite a steep learning curve, an unintuitive UI and no tutorial. — [Roundtable Co-op (Feb 2024)](https://roundtablecoop.com/reviews/carrier-command-2-review-complex-and-challenging/)
- Steam sentiment is about 68% positive. It is "best experienced with 2-4 players." Reportedly up to 16 can join, but "seven of them don't really have anything to do." There is no formal captain role and no AI delegation, so the crew self-organises. — [Vaporlens](https://vaporlens.app/app/1489630/carrier_command_2); [Co-Optimus](https://www.co-optimus.com/game/10384/pc/carrier-command-2.html)

**Barotrauma (FakeFish/Undertow)**
- The captain's job: drive the sub, monitor the nav console, call orders and facilitate teamwork. The captain is "pretty much stapled to the nav console" and must set the autopilot whenever leaving it. The captain's ID opens almost every door, and there is only one captain per ship. — [Steam guide "What the duck do I do?"](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1769970362); [Official wiki: Jobs](https://barotraumagame.com/wiki/Jobs)
- Orders go through a radial menu, which directs AI crew. Orders can be issued pre-emptively (for example "fight intruders" before any appear), and up to 3 can be saved for quick reassignment. — [Official wiki: Orders](https://barotraumagame.com/wiki/Orders)

**Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes (Steel Crate, 2015)**
- The defuser sees the bomb but not the manual, and the experts see the manual but not the bomb. "It starts with balance." "The fun really lives between players." "We're making a communications-based party game, it just happens to have bombs in it." — [Game Developer, 14 Mar 2016](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/finding-the-fun-in-bomb-defusal-with-i-keep-talking-and-nobody-explodes-i-); [GDC Vault](https://gdcvault.com/play/1023471/Designing-Asymmetric-Gameplay-For-Keep)

### Inferences
- Across the shooter hybrids, the seat's appeal is leverage: one action (a scan, a supply drop, a spawn point) visibly helps many people. Designs that make the leverage legible, such as BF4's HVT or HLL's Airhead spawn, deliver the reward even to commanders who are not tactically brilliant.
- The fun of the captain seat in Artemis and KTANE is social. It depends on a co-located group, so it does not carry over to strangers online.

### Gaps
- Empires (Source mod): I found no reliable primary or review source on its commander design.
- No developer-published data on which share of players ever commands, in any title.

## Why is it often the least-filled role, and which fixes worked?

### Takeaway
The seat goes unfilled because of overload, being a full-time non-shooter in a shooter, needing the team's cooperation without any authority over it, bearing blame for team failure, and poor onboarding. Fixes that demonstrably shipped include AI commander bots (NS2), builders taking over construction when no commander is present (Savage 2), turning the seat into a full-time but richer job with RTS-style counterplay (BF4), skill ratings for commanders (NS2), and DICE's opposite fix in BF3: delete the seat and push its powers down to squad leaders.

### Cited Findings
- NS2: players report games often have no commander, or the commander leaves and nobody replaces them, and the match declines. Many players "join to shoot things." The game doesn't teach the role, and some don't know it exists. — [Steam "No commander"](https://steamcommunity.com/app/4920/discussions/0/610573751149960116/); [Steam "I wish there was no commander role"](https://steamcommunity.com/app/4920/discussions/0/828925849512173303/)
- NS2: the marine commander is generally considered harder in public games, because marines are more vulnerable to rushes. — [Unknown Worlds forum guide](https://forums.unknownworlds.com/discussion/131205/natural-selection-2-a-complete-guide-updating-soon)
- NS2: players can practise via console commands against bots with autobuild. — [Steam "Commander Practice"](https://steamcommunity.com/app/4920/discussions/0/359543542247273198/)
- BF2 (DICE's diagnosis, 22 Sep 2011): the seat was "a bit flawed." Only one person per team could play it, "it was always the highest ranking one," and commanders "basically spent the whole match lying on the ground on some obscure corner of the map." BF3 pushed artillery and recon functions to squad leaders and gadgets so players could "fill the roles that the Commander had, but then also be an active soldier" (Karl Magnus Troedsson). — [Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/70295/dice-explains-why-battlefield-3-has-no-commander)
- Shacknews readers noted that half the time people wouldn't listen to the commander, which made the role feel idle. This is reader opinion, not a DICE statement. — [Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/70295/dice-explains-why-battlefield-3-has-no-commander)
- BF4 brought the seat back as full-time, with tablet access. The mobile app was dropped in April 2015. The seat did not return in BF1, BFV, 2042 or BF6, and I found no official DICE statement explaining why. — [Battlefield.com](https://www.battlefield.com/news/battlefield-4-commander-app-out-now-on-android-and-ios-1); [EA forums](https://forums.ea.com/discussions/battlefield-6-general-discussion-en/why-does-bf-development-always-remove-popular-features/12458994); [TechRadar on 2042](https://www.techradar.com/news/battlefield-2042-has-lost-one-of-its-biggest-selling-points)
- Digit's BF4 review: the mode's success depends heavily on whether the commander is competent and whether squads listen. — [Digit](https://www.digit.in/reviews/general/battlefield-4-pc-review-3432.html)
- Squad forums: "#1 issue is being overloaded." The commander must choose between leading a squad and commanding. Assets are few, with long rearm intervals. The role "does not have the ability to support the SLs and reward them for following orders." Players tell the commander to "shut up and just give them artillery," treating the seat as the "UAV and artillery" operator. "You cannot make 50 internet players do anything at all." — [Squad forums](https://forums.joinsquad.com/discussion/774/commander-role); [Steam: "Commander Role is absolutely essential"](https://steamcommunity.com/app/393380/discussions/6/1696048786956529920/); [Steam: "Commander Role"](https://steamcommunity.com/app/393380/discussions/6/1753520327890191260/)
- HLL: command chat fills with "we need a commander... does anyone want to jump in?" A player reports "half the times I've played Commander are the times I only meant to drop my team a supply drop or a tank and I end up being roped into playing the role the whole match." The command chat "adds another level of complexity and can be extremely distracting." — [Sheepdog's Scroll Substack](https://thesheepdogscroll.substack.com/p/hll-6-leadership-intro-learning-to)
- HLL: the commander depends on others. Nodes exist only if an Engineer lays and builds them. Without a good commander, a match "can quickly devolve into a frustrating, aimless mess." Players say spending munitions on recon and bombing runs upsets command chat when they need supply. — [The Loadout](https://theloadout.com/hell-let-loose/commander-guide); [Steam HLL thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/686810/discussions/0/2843417619897410730)
- Squad community proposals include a 4-5 player non-combat logistics or support command squad (building FOBs, running supplies), and giving the commander "teeth" to reward squad leaders. — [Squad forums](https://forums.joinsquad.com/discussion/774/commander-role); [Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/393380/discussions/6/1742260304699111800/)
- Carrier Command 2: an absent delegation or AI layer means extra players are idle ("seven of them don't really have anything to do"). This is the idle-time failure mode in reverse: too many hands for too few stations. — [Co-Optimus](https://www.co-optimus.com/game/10384/pc/carrier-command-2.html)

### Inferences
- The fixes that worked share one trait: they lower the cost of the seat being empty or bad, through AI bots, builder fallback, or decentralised call-ins. Making the seat more powerful does not fill it.
- The HLL quote ("I only meant to drop a supply drop...") shows strong demand for short commander stints with low commitment. A design that lets someone take the seat for one action or one phase, then hand back to an autopilot, targets the main avoidance driver directly.
- Rewarding squad leaders for following orders, as BF4 did with points for completed orders and squad promotion, is the fix Squad players explicitly ask for. It addresses the "no authority" complaint.

### Gaps
- No quantified data on fill rates, or on whether BF4's or NS2's changes measurably raised them.
- I found no direct developer statement on NS2 commander toxicity or blame. Cleveland interviews predate release.

## How does the commander's input become visible and felt by other players?

### Takeaway
The best-felt inputs are physical, in-world objects with clear beneficiaries and attribution: crates landing on your squad, a spawn point appearing, artillery hitting a marked area, a promotion bar jumping, an HVT marker on everyone's HUD. Inputs that only reach the commander, such as BF2's satellite scan, depend on voice relay and are easily lost.

### Cited Findings
- BF2 satellite scan showed enemies only to the commander, who had to relay positions by text or voice, so the intel's value depended on comms. — [GameSpot preview](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/battlefield-2-hands-on-on-foot-and-commander-view/1100-6114811/); [AnandTech](https://forums.anandtech.com/threads/battlefield-2-strategies-commander-mode-explained.1621302/)
- BF4 made outputs land in-world for others: HVT marks shown to the whole team (and an alert to the enemy) for 45 s; AC-130 as a spawn others physically ride in; squad promotion pushing unlock bars; halved squad respawn; crates allowing class switching. The enemy commander can see where you scanned, and EMP UAVs can intercept cruise missiles, which creates visible commander-vs-commander counterplay. — [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/battlefield/battlefield-4-commander-mode-gives-load-powers-player-hot-seat); [Dignitas](https://dignitas.gg/articles/blogs/Battlefield/4209/battlefield-4-a-field-guide-part-1-whats-new)
- BF4's strongest assets are gated on ground players capturing objectives (launch pads for cruise missiles, typically the centre point for the AC-130). Ground play feeds the commander, and the commander pays it back. — [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/battlefield/battlefield-4-commander-mode-gives-load-powers-player-hot-seat); [Dignitas](https://dignitas.gg/articles/blogs/Battlefield/4209/battlefield-4-a-field-guide-part-1-whats-new)
- Nuclear Dawn: commander research and buildings unlock new abilities for other players' classes, so the commander's decisions show up as new options in soldiers' kits. — [Wikipedia: Nuclear Dawn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_Dawn)
- Savage: officer promotion makes the commander's trust visible as passive buffs around the officer. Buildings require team gold, which action players can donate. — [Wikipedia: Savage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savage:_The_Battle_for_Newerth); [Wikipedia: Savage 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savage_2:_A_Tortured_Soul)
- HLL: Airhead drops a temporary spawn and Reinforce boosts capture strength in a sector, both felt immediately on the front. — [HLL Fandom](https://hellletloose.fandom.com/wiki/Commander)
- Artemis: the captain's input is purely verbal, and stations execute it. "Players decide when, if, and how loudly to relay info." — [Goodreads review](https://www.goodreads.com/author_blog_posts/3853105-game-review-artemis); [Tom's Guide](https://tomsguide.com/us/review-artemis-bridge-simulator,review-1928.html)
- KTANE: information is valuable only when communicated. The design keeps inputs "simple and deliberate" so failures are about team decisions, and rules unambiguous (capital "O" removed because it looked like zero). — [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/finding-the-fun-in-bomb-defusal-with-i-keep-talking-and-nobody-explodes-i-)

### Inferences
- For a design where the commander supplies intel to Lieutenant and Logistics players, intel should arrive as in-world artefacts in the recipient's own mode (marked targets, revealed fog, a buff tag naming the commander) rather than as chat. BF2's private scan is the cautionary example, and BF4's team-visible HVT is the fix.
- Gating the strongest commander tools on other players' achievements (BF4 launch pads, HLL engineer-built nodes) creates a felt loop between modes. That maps naturally onto Logistics feeding the Commander's budget.

### Gaps
- No telemetry on how often relayed scans or orders were acted on.

## Does the commander work in real time or in planning phases, and what are the effects of each?

### Takeaway
Every shooter-hybrid commander surveyed works in continuous real time, which produces overload, idle downtime between cooldowns, and comms burden. No surveyed game uses discrete planning phases for its commander seat. The closest analogues are pre-emptive or standing orders (Barotrauma saved and pre-emptive orders), economy cooldowns (HLL resources per minute), and KTANE's tuned real-time tension.

### Cited Findings
- HLL: resources tick at 30 per minute plus node bonuses, and abilities have cooldowns, so "the question is rarely whether to use them but when." Players say even full nodes can't sustain Encouraged, Airhead and Reinforce every 10 minutes. — [Guildorder](https://guildorder.com/games/hell_let_loose/wiki/commander-role-and-strategic-abilities); [Steam HLL thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/686810/discussions/0/2843417619897410730)
- Squad players complain of long rearm intervals, meaning idle time between call-ins. — [Squad forums](https://forums.joinsquad.com/discussion/774/commander-role)
- BF2: commanders lay prone in a corner of the map all match, which signals passive, idle time in the real-time seat. — [Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/70295/dice-explains-why-battlefield-3-has-no-commander)
- Barotrauma: orders can be issued pre-emptively, and up to 3 saved for quick reassignment. The captain is tied to the nav console in real time and must engage the autopilot to step away. — [Barotrauma wiki: Orders](https://barotraumagame.com/wiki/Orders); [Steam guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1769970362)
- KTANE pacing: a hidden system scores how well players are doing and adds disruptions (alarms, lights out) when they are doing well. The music uses 30-second loops rising to maximum pressure at T-30. The aim is to avoid a death spiral and produce last-second saves: "We don't want to accelerate a death spiral; we want people to get those really exciting moments." — [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/finding-the-fun-in-bomb-defusal-with-i-keep-talking-and-nobody-explodes-i-); [GDC 2016 slides](https://media.gdcvault.com/gdc2016/Presentations/Kane_Ben_Designing_Asymmetric_Gameplay.pdf)
- Carrier Command 2: real-time pressure from an advancing enemy, with high UI complexity. — [Roundtable Co-op](https://roundtablecoop.com/reviews/carrier-command-2-review-complex-and-challenging/)

### Inferences
- The asker's plan (night = strategic war map, day = support call-ins) has no direct precedent among these titles. It answers their two biggest documented pain points. Planning phases remove real-time overload and the need for live voice comms, and the day call-ins give the commander something to do while others play, which reduces BF2-style idle time. The risk is that day call-ins rebuild the "UAV and artillery operator" feel that Squad players resent, unless they tie back to the night plan.
- Budgeted call-ins with an economy (HLL) prevent spam but create waiting. The day phase should give the commander low-stakes intel or logistics micro-tasks between call-ins rather than pure cooldown waiting.
- KTANE's adaptive pacing (adding disruption when the team is winning, never feeding a death spiral) is directly reusable for a shared day/night clock.

### Gaps
- I found no game with a turn-based or phase-based commander seat sitting alongside real-time players in these titles to cite as direct evidence.

## How do these games handle an absent or bad commander (AI fallback, auto-commander, ejection)?

### Takeaway
There are four patterns: ejection or mutiny votes (BF2/2142/BF4, NS2, Savage), AI commander bots (NS2, plus mods for BF2), fallback of commander powers to ground roles (Savage 2 builders, BF3's removal of the seat), and voluntary step-down. Barotrauma's autopilot shows the same fallback at the scale of a single console.

### Cited Findings
- BF2/2142: teammates can start a mutiny vote from the squad screen to remove an inactive or poor commander, which reopens the post. — [Wikipedia: BF2142](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battlefield_2142); [AnandTech](https://forums.anandtech.com/threads/battlefield-2-strategies-commander-mode-explained.1621302/)
- BF4: a squad leader can start a mutiny vote to remove the commander. — [Battlefield wiki: Commander (Feature)](https://battlefield.fandom.com/wiki/Commander_(Feature)) (page summarised via search; fetch blocked)
- NS2: there is an eject-commander vote (server command `sv_eject`). The Shine admin mod defaults eject to 50% of the team. — [NS2 Console Commands wiki](https://naturalselection.fandom.com/wiki/Console_Commands); [Shine Base Commands](https://github.com/Person8880/Shine/wiki/Base-Commands)
- NS2 commander bots: update 301 (Jun 2016) improved alien commander bots' resource management and had marine bots research the MG. Shine supports a vote for commander bots after a delay (default 300 s). Build 303 notes: commander bots can be replaced by a human entering the command station, and bots were wrongly counted in concede and eject votes (fixed). — [GamingOnLinux, 7 Jun 2016](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2016/06/natural-selection-2-adds-a-new-map-and-smarter-bots/); [Shine wiki](https://github.com/Person8880/Shine/wiki/Base-Commands); [NS wiki: Build 303](https://naturalselection.fandom.com/wiki/Patches_(Natural_Selection_2)/Build_303)
- BF2 community mods added an AI commander or auto-artillery plugin. These are fan mods, not official. — [ModDB: Advanced AI commander](https://www.moddb.com/games/battlefield-2/addons/advanced-ai-commander-plugin); [ModDB: Auto Artillery](https://www.moddb.com/games/battlefield-2/addons/auto-artilleryai-commander-plugin)
- Savage: the commander may be impeached by popular vote. In Savage 2 the commander can step down to fight, and if a team has no commander, builder units gain build ability until someone takes the seat. — [Wikipedia: Savage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savage:_The_Battle_for_Newerth); [Wikipedia: Savage 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savage_2:_A_Tortured_Soul)
- HLL: a departing commander can be replaced by anyone taking the slot (forum report). Players have proposed a squad-leader vote in which the vote starter volunteers to take over. That is a proposal, not shipped. — [Steam HLL thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/686810/discussions/0/594019565986408852); [Steam HLL thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/686810/discussions/0/3196988875699215162)
- Nuclear Dawn: commander chosen at random. I found no source on its fallback behaviour. — [Wikipedia: Nuclear Dawn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_Dawn)
- Barotrauma: the captain must set the autopilot when leaving the nav console, which acts as an in-seat AI fallback. — [Steam guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1769970362)
- BF2's rank-based selection ("always the highest ranking one") is itself listed by DICE as a flaw. — [Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/70295/dice-explains-why-battlefield-3-has-no-commander)

### Inferences
- NS2's "human can take over from a bot at any time" is the closest shipped precedent to the asker's autopilot-fills-empty-roles design. The Build 303 bug (bots counted as voters) is a reminder to exclude autopilots from team votes and stats.
- Mutiny votes target bad commanders but amplify blame culture. Lower-drama alternatives are Savage 2-style step-down and power fallback: the autopilot or other modes absorb commander duties, so a human commander's absence never stalls the team.
- For a premium co-op game with friends, ejection matters less than an easy hand-back to autopilot and a "commander suggests, autopilot executes if no one objects" model, consistent with KTANE's advice that failures should feel like shared team decisions rather than one person's fault.

### Gaps
- No data on how often mutiny or eject votes were used, or on their effect on retention.
- BF4 mutiny specifics (thresholds, cooldowns) are unconfirmed because the wiki fetch failed.
- Hell Let Loose and Squad: I found no official source on a shipped AI-commander fallback or commander-removal vote.
