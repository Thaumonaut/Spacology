# Voxel rendering: shadows and grass

Headless captures of `prototypes/microvoxel-world.html?extent=infinite&seed=2718&cx=-30&cz=-32&lx=4&lz=4` (1280×675, paused, so no wind motion), except where noted.

| `2-sun-shadows.png` | Ground-only hillshade plus the sun shadow map: trees cast soft cone shadows. Only things standing on the ground cast; terrain never does. |
| `3-blade-grass.png` | First grass pass: small five-blade tufts with flat colour. |
| `4-stylized-grass.png` | Stylized grass: seven long curved blades per clump, roots matched to a shared meadow colour field on the ground, sunny yellow-green tips, soft shadows. Wind gusts lean the blades and brighten their tips (live only). |

Close-up crowns lifted to match their far-zoom colour; grass ground shifts to the blades' average colour once the blades fade out. |

| `17-ghibli-clouds-preview.png` | The cloud layer alone over a flat green at a 700 m span (headless runs cannot reach continent zoom in reasonable time). Same noise as the cloud shadows, so each cloud floats over its shadow. Opacity slider in the toolbar (default 85%). |

Fixes clouds swapping near the world origin (the frame wrap was at zero; it is now centred on zero). |
| `19-ink-outlines.png` | Ink outlines: the scene renders into an offscreen 4× multisampled target, then a full-screen pass inks the near side of every depth jump above a zoom-scaled threshold (about 1 m up close) in a deep cool tint of the surface colour.
