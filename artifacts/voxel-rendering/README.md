# Voxel rendering: shadows and grass

Headless captures of `prototypes/microvoxel-world.html?extent=infinite&seed=2718&cx=-30&cz=-32&lx=4&lz=4` (1280×675, paused, so no wind motion), except where noted.

| File | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| `1-before-halo.png` | Main checkout with the first-pass hillshade: tree canopies counted as terrain, so a false ring circles the giant tree and smudges sit beside every tree. |
| `2-sun-shadows.png` | Ground-only hillshade plus the sun shadow map: trees cast soft cone shadows. Only things standing on the ground cast; terrain never does. |
| `3-blade-grass.png` | First grass pass: small five-blade tufts with flat colour. |
| `4-stylized-grass.png` | Stylized grass: seven long curved blades per clump, roots matched to a shared meadow colour field on the ground, sunny yellow-green tips, soft shadows. Wind gusts lean the blades and brighten their tips (live only). |
| `5-ridge-142m.png` | `cell=0.125&cx=39&cz=-151` at 142 m: crystal spires cast onto the ice; no stair moiré. |
| `6-anime-first-pass.png` | Anime pass 1: crowns lit by a smoothed canopy-shape normal in three flat bands (sunny yellow-green, leaf green, teal shadow); simpler two-colour grass. The light/shadow split reads as a straight line down each cone. |
| `7-anime-crowns-soft-edges.png` | Crowns broken into leafy puffs; a grass cover map gives a noisy, frayed border where grass meets other ground, with shorter, sparser tufts at meadow edges. The bare ring under the giant tree awaits the generator's podzol. |
| `8-leaf-cards.png` | Leaf cards: camera-facing leaf clumps over each crown's outer blocks, cut to a fluffy outline, filled with small cell-pattern leaves and lit by the crown's outward direction in the anime bands. The blocks underneath sink to a deep teal interior. |
| `9-leaf-cards-blended.png` | Cards drawn a card-width nearer the camera so their own crown's blocks no longer cut them off; block faces that show through carry the same small-leaf pattern in deeper tones. Crowns read as continuous foliage. |
| `10-fir-sprigs-base-colour.png` | Conifer cards are upright fir sprigs (narrow top, drooping needle skirt) held close to the crown, so cones keep their shape. Bands rebalanced toward the base green: highlights only on the sunniest puffs and softened toward the base. |
| `11-conifer-clumps-restored.png` | Fir sprigs rejected (the triangles read worse than the leafy clumps). Conifers use the round leafy clumps again, smaller and held closer to the crown; the base-green balance from 10 stays. |
| `12a-conifers-mixed.png`, `12b-conifers-round-soft.png` | Two zoom-ins closer, `cx=-31&cz=-32`. Conifer card outline per card: `&leaves=mixed` (round, soft triangle or hard triangle; the default) vs `&leaves=round+soft`. Each card now shades as a small tuft (lit toward the sun and its top, darker at base and rim), has a finer, softer leaf pattern and antialiased edges. Also try `round`, `soft` or `hard`. |
| `13-conifer-forest-grass.png` | One zoom-in, `cx=-31&cz=-32`. Grass and ground take the forest's tone from a per-node conifer map (biome + temperature on a 16 m world grid): deep blue-green here among conifers, bright warm green among broadleaf trees. Close-up crowns lifted to match their far-zoom colour; grass ground shifts to the blades' average colour once the blades fade out. |
| `14-trunks-hidden-in-crowns.png` | Bark above 45% of a tree's height (trunk tips poking through crowns, the reddish freckles across distant forests) is painted as deep crown interior; lower trunks, including the giant's, keep their bark. |
| `15a-rocks-before.png`, `15b-rocks-anime.png` | `cx=-137&cz=14`, one zoom-out. Basalt, stone, sandstone and gravel in three cel bands: base grey dominant, cool lavender-blue shadow faces, warm highlights only on strongly sunlit faces, ridges a little brighter, soft facet patches. (A first, brighter attempt washed the boulders out nearly white.) |
| `16-cloud-shadows-steady-cards.png` | Drifting cloud shadows (about 100 m patches, cast along the sun; a patch edge darkens the giant's crown here). Leaf cards no longer flicker where clumps overlap: sway moves them on screen only, the forward pull is half a card, and a fixed per-card nudge breaks depth ties. |
| `17-ghibli-clouds-preview.png` | The cloud layer alone over a flat green at a 700 m span (headless runs cannot reach continent zoom in reasonable time). Ghibli cumulus: masses scalloped into cauliflower lobes at two scales, always-golden-hour light (cream and peach sunward cheeks, a golden rim, lavender-blue lee sides), faint wisps. Same noise as the cloud shadows, so each cloud floats over its shadow. Opacity slider in the toolbar (default 85%). |
| `18-cloud-banks-wisps.png` | Cloud layer alone at an 1800 m span. A slow ~900 m weather pattern gathers clouds into large banks with wide clear sky between; thin wind-streaked wisps scatter around the bank fringes. Cloud shapes and shadows share one field. Fixes clouds swapping near the world origin (the frame wrap was at zero; it is now centred on zero). |
| `19-ink-outlines.png` | Ink outlines: the scene renders into an offscreen 4× multisampled target, then a full-screen pass inks the near side of every depth jump above a zoom-scaled threshold (about 1 m up close) in a deep cool tint of the surface colour. Trees and crowns get silhouettes; grass and small steps do not. Clouds draw after the pass. `?outlines=0` compares without; Economy skips the pass. |
| `20a-outlines-soft-thick.png` | Outlines about two pixels wide (two rings of eight samples, scaled by the device pixel ratio) with a feathered outer fringe and a lighter cool tint. Minimum threshold raised to 1.2 m so single grass blades do not ink; flowers are excluded (they coarsen into dark specks at mid zoom). |
| `20b-bob-ross-tiers.png` | Three zoom-outs. Conifer crowns shade as five or six stacked bough skirts (sunlit top, dark drooping underside), spaced by the tree's own height found by searching the canopy map around each point. Geometry is still a smooth cone; the vegetation agent is adding matching notched tiers at the same spacing. |
| `21a-snowy-trees.png` | After rebasing onto the merged terrain (`cx=41&cz=-108`). Trees standing on snow or ice carry snow on their upward-facing lobes and tier tops, from a per-column snow cover in the biome map. |
| `21b-calmer-broadleaf.png` | `cx=55&cz=46`, two zoom-outs. Broadleaf crowns no longer read as broccoli: lobes about three times larger, weaker lobe contrast, softer band edges. |
| `21c-rebased-new-world.png` | The rendering pass on the merged terrain: notched conifer tiers, bushes, ruins and the new grasses, all with leaf cards, blades, shadows and outlines. |

The flat dark ring around the giant tree in 2–4 is a different ground material from the terrain generator, so no grass grows there.
