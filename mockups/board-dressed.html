What is on it

1
Turn order, with the Bloom in itNames and waits, coloured by side. A broken enemy dims. Entities that are not units sit in the queue too.
2
Enemy intentWho each one will hit, above its card. Area attacks read differently from single ones. Into the Breach’s rule: never surprise the player with something they could have planned around.
3
Weakness pips, lit or dimTwo per enemy, bright when someone aboard can exploit it. The pre-fight read kept on screen during the fight.
4
Shields as rings, ailments as dotsLayers are concentric rings outside the card; stacks are dots at the corner. Both countable at a glance, no text.
5
Recovery score with band marksHow much of the board you have taken, thresholds drawn on. Tells you what one more kill is worth.
6
On field and off field, splitWith counts, a downed crewmate dimmed rather than removed, and the strip target living where the crew already is.
7
Context stripWhere you are, how dangerous, which conditions are live, which tags are active. Everything that changes the rules, in one line.
8
Charged crew marked READYA unit that can interrupt is outlined amber in the tray, so an available ultimate never has to be hunted for.
9
The ability named on the actorThe acting card says what it is about to do, not just who it is.
10
A 44px inspect cornerGlyph small inside it. The rest of the card stays draggable.

Green numbers are layers the rough draft did not have. The rest are refinements to what was already there.

What is deliberately kept off

—
Numeric stats on every cardAttack, defence and speed live in the inspect sheet. On the board they would compete with the cards, which is the one thing Snap’s rule forbids.
—
A settings or menu barPause belongs in a corner, off the play surface. Nothing that is not the game holds a permanent position.
—
Element colour on crew cardsTag colour already does that work. Two colour systems on one card is one too many.
—
Damage previews for your own crewYou do not pick their targets, so it would be noise. Enemy intent matters because you can still move people out of it.
