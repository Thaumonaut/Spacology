How it works

1
Packs are themed two waysSome by mechanic — rot and bloom, marks and answers, shields and reflect. Some by element pair. So there are two ways to aim a purchase, and they slice the pool differently.
2
Rarity is visible before you buyThe bar chart is the pack’s actual composition, and the line under it names the best card inside: “shows 1 PRIME”. You never buy blind, so the decision is real rather than a gamble.
3
Fit is stated against your crew“you have Ash and Pyre aboard”, or “nothing aboard leans either way”. The game does the cross-reference so you do not have to hold it in your head.
4
The dilemma, in one lineThe prime is in the pack you do not want. That sentence under the offers is the whole store: take the strong card off-theme, take the right theme with nothing special, or pay to look again.
5
Refresh costs, and risesRerolling the offers is the third option and it has a price that climbs. So “hold out for the right prime” is a real gamble with a real cost, not a free spin.
6
Odds climb as the voyage goes1 in 40 early, 1 in 6 deep, and deep packs at double cost carry 1 in 3. A prime landing in act three is expected; one landing in act one is a run-defining piece of luck.

Rarity

◆
Commonthe working roster — one clear job, done reliably
◆
Uncommona second property, usually a small one
◆
Rarechanges how a tag plays rather than how hard it hits
◆
Primea rule of its own. One prime can decide what the rest of the crew is for.

Four tiers, and the jump that matters is rare to prime. A prime is not a bigger number, it is a rule — which is why seeing one in a pack is worth rerolling for, and why taking one off-theme is a genuine mistake rather than a small loss.
