What is on it

1
Voyage status, same column as the battle’s queueThe screen never reorganises between phases. Gold, integrity and streak stack where the turn order sits in a fight, and the foot shows what a clean leg would pay.
2
Crates as the only primary actionThree tiles, card counts in the corner, price in amber. The focused crate names the tag it leans toward rather than saying “your tags”.
3
The bay, with Keep and Strip as buttons32px targets, and the strip button states the shards. Tapping a card to destroy it is the kind of thing that gets done once by accident and never trusted again.
4
Commission, collapsed and lit when it mattersOne row. It turns green and reads “one short” only when you are on a threshold — the moment it is worth 74 gold.
5
Forge and hold as two quiet rowsShard counts by colour, crystals ready called out in amber, and the trade rate stated so it is never a thing you have to remember.
6
The same tray as combatIdentical component, so the crew you are shopping for is visible while you shop. The empty slot reads “needs Crew” rather than “empty”. The strip target lives here.
