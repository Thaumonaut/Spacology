Store layouts

Three directions for the quartermaster, before any of it gets built. Same content in each: crates, the pull, the crew, the hold, and a way out.

The current store stacks six panels in one scroll with no hierarchy, and the inspect target is 15 px inside a draggable card. Apple's minimum is 44 px. Both are fixed in all three below.

A · Tabbed one job at a time

Recruit, Salvage and Crew become tabs. Nothing scrolls past a screen, and the tab bar tells you what the store even contains — which the current version never does.

The fix is not a bigger dot. It is an invisible 44 px corner zone with a small glyph inside it, so the target is thumb-sized while the card still looks clean. The rest of the card stays draggable.

Or drop the corner entirely: a short press opens the sheet, a drag moves the unit. One finger, no target to miss — but it needs a hint the first time, because nothing on screen advertises it.

What I would build

C for the store, because the whole point of the screen is filling a gap in the crew and C is the only one where the gap is visible while you shop. Take A's explicit Keep / Strip buttons on pulled cards — tapping a card to destroy it is the kind of thing that gets done by accident once and then never trusted again. Take B's collapsed commission row, since commissioning is rare and does not deserve permanent space.

Then the 44 px corner zone everywhere, and the trash slot under the crew.
