# Spacology portrait art direction

## Shared visual language

Soft painterly anime/JRPG portraiture with mature proportions, expressive draftsmanship,
restrained linework, warm ivory paper grain, tactile brush texture, and a simple circular
elemental field behind the subject. The style references govern rendering only. They must
never overwrite an individual character's complexion, hair, age, facial structure, species,
clothing, or silhouette.

Element fields are fixed: Order is cyan-blue, Chaos rose-crimson, Growth green-teal,
Decay sepia-ochre, Energy amber-gold, and Void deep violet.

## Species rules

- A minor demi-human is human-forward with selective animal traits.
- A major demi-human is creature-forward while preserving an expressive JRPG character read.
- Mana-born use clearly nonhuman blue skin rather than a human complexion.
- Moth-folk are consistently very pale: moon-pale skin or fur, powdery antennae, and patterned
  moth wings. They do not inherit Dr Stella Anuye's complexion or hair.
- Dr Stella Anuye is a style anchor, not a universal character template.

## Prompt set

Portrait prompt: square bust portrait; one named lore character; explicit species, age,
complexion, hair, facial structure, clothing, equipment, and element; soft painterly anime/JRPG
rendering; warm paper texture; flat circular field in the character's element; no text, frame,
logo, watermark, photorealism, glossy 3D rendering, or identity leakage from another character.

Enemy prompt: square field-guide portrait; one of the eight mechanical archetypes; a silhouette
that communicates its mechanic at thumbnail size; organic science-fantasy anatomy; the same soft
painterly anime/JRPG rendering and circular elemental field; no text or interface.

Splash prompt: landscape title background; the living research vessel ARC 017 - Star Singer over an alien
reef-world; subtle presence of all six elements; the Null shown as erased image and starlight;
ship and planet weighted to the right with quiet title space on the left; no baked-in typography.
