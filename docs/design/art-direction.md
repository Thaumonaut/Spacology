# Spacology portrait art direction

## Shared visual language

Soft painterly anime/JRPG portraiture with mature proportions, expressive draftsmanship,
restrained linework, warm ivory paper grain, tactile brush texture, and a simple circular
elemental field behind the subject. The style references govern rendering only. They must
never overwrite an individual character's complexion, hair, age, facial structure, species,
clothing, or silhouette.

Element fields are fixed: Order is cyan-blue, Chaos rose-crimson, Growth green-teal,
Decay sepia-ochre, Energy amber-gold, and Void deep violet.

## Species rules

- There is no major/minor or demi/semi-human hierarchy. Every playable character has a humanoid,
  expressive JRPG read while their lineage changes silhouette, biology and sensory traits.
- Mana-born use clearly nonhuman blue skin rather than a human complexion.
- Moth-folk are consistently very pale: moon-pale skin or fur, powdery antennae, and patterned
  moth wings. They do not inherit Dr Stella Anuye's complexion or hair.
- Fungal people remain recognizably humanoid but use unique mushroom crowns, gills, hyphae and
  fruiting patterns instead of conventional hair.
- Dr Stella Anuye is a style anchor, not a universal character template.

## Prompt set

Portrait prompt: square bust portrait; one named lore character; explicit species, age,
complexion, hair, facial structure, clothing, equipment, and element; soft painterly anime/JRPG
rendering; warm paper texture; flat circular field in the character's element; no text, frame,
logo, watermark, photorealism, glossy 3D rendering, or identity leakage from another character.

Enemy prompt: square field-guide portrait; one of the eight mechanical archetypes; a silhouette
that communicates its mechanic at thumbnail size; organic science-fantasy anatomy; the same soft
painterly anime/JRPG rendering and circular elemental field; no text or interface.

Splash prompt: landscape title background; the living research vessel ARC 017 - Star Singer over an alien
reef-world; subtle presence of all six elements; the Null shown as erased image and starlight;
ship and planet weighted to the right with quiet title space on the left; no baked-in typography.

## Current portrait refinements — Aether Weavers

Human facial structure and body proportions can coexist with strong biological lineage traits. Do not reduce bird-folk or amphiboid identities to ordinary humans wearing decorations. Roonie has a human teenage face and body, large feathery axolotl gills, speckled amphibian skin and fin-shaped ears instead of human ears. Veska has a human face, strong feathered crest and plumage, feathered limbs and a wing; no beak or animal head. Abike, Coda, Imke and Bosk are useful references for this balance. Ivara is an explicit exception: preserve his original synthetic narwhal design.

Warm paper is the background foundation. Element circles, restrained botanical ornament and star-map tracery are welcome, provided they stay secondary to the face and silhouette. Avoid busy scenery, architecture and framing machinery. A completely blank backdrop is not required. Preserve Roonie’s original crouched maintenance pose, wrench, conduit and small robot.

Roonie’s final activity is conduit repair: his eyes and both hands attend the same job. The small robot rests beside him. His background uses paper, a muted amber circle and a few sparse arcs/dots. Veska’s green botanical circle and Silen’s blue survey circle remain valid background complexity references.

Daven is a marble stoneskin. His bald crown, face, beard, moustache, eyebrows and remaining hair are all mineral, with natural gray veining. Hair reads as sculptural stone curls and ridges, never organic strands. Keep his broad fat/strong build and active braced barrier-repair pose.

Rockskin anatomy follows the supplied rockskin reference: broad angular interlocking planes, layered fracture edges and slab-like mineral locks. Daven uses this anatomy in veined marble. Avoid tiny pebble mosaics or organic hair painted gray.
