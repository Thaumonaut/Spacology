# Future expansion ideas: fantasy peoples and factions

Status: **deferred at the user's request; possible future expansion only.** Goblins, undead, vampires and the other new enemy types below are outside the current run, rewards and equipment work. Preserve these ideas for later consideration; they are not established lore, approved additions or implementation requirements.

Treasure carriers remain in the current design. “Loot goblin” means a treasure-hoarding enemy that drops valuables on death, whatever its species; it can use an existing faction without introducing any of these new peoples.

## Possible future direction

Add recognizable fantasy identities alongside the existing alien ecologies. Keep their familiar names, silhouettes and useful gameplay expectations, then give them cultures and a place in the setting's ordinary mana-based technology. They should be identifiable as goblins, undead and vampires without needing to read a lore paragraph first.

Follow `world.md`'s distinction between species and culture. A goblin people is not automatically one enemy faction; undead describes a condition that could affect several peoples. Friendly specialists, traders and future crew can share these identities with hostile encounters. Preserve the enemy catalogue's recovery premise: Null corruption can distort these societies' behavior, just as it affects existing factions. Different hostile motives would be a separate story decision.

Do not rename the Ossuary Reef into undead. Its living bone ecology is already a distinct idea. Actual skeletons, revenants and ghosts would introduce a different history and visual language.

## Three expansion candidates

| Identity | Proposed faction expression | Visual language | Combat identity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Goblins | Salvage clans and travelling workshops | Small bodies, large ears, expressive faces, oversized equipment and visibly repaired tools | Numerous weak scrappers supported by dangerous contraptions |
| Undead | A revenant community whose bodies and memories are maintained through ritual and instruments | Skeletons, preserved bodies, spectral figures, worn personal belongings and memorial markings | Telegraph a limited reanimation; prioritize the source or finish the return |
| Vampires | Nocturnal houses with blood-based medicine and long-lived social institutions | Recognizable fangs, predatory elegance, blood vessels and protective travelling attire | Recover health from successful attacks; protection and breaks deny their sustain |

Names for the individual factions, their homeworlds and their histories remain open. These are not replacements for the existing catalogue.

### Goblins

Basic fights can use weak scrappers and slingers. Swarms use only those small, weak bodies, without elites, armored machines or hidden leader buffs that turn them into durable enemies. An elite encounter could feature a mechanic directing one dangerous device. A boss could be an overgrown salvage machine with distinct parts and phases.

Friendly goblin workshops offer a natural home for equipment disassembly, reforging and trading. A loot carrier could be a goblin with an overloaded pack, but greed or treasure carrying is not an innate trait shared by every goblin.

### Undead

Begin with skeleton crews, a revenant guardian and a reanimator elite. Give each eligible enemy at most one clearly marked return for the first prototype. Disabling the reanimator prevents future returns; otherwise an ordinary crew can handle the finite second form. Do not require a holy element, rare cleansing item or special character.

Recovery points and loot belong to the original enemy identity and are awarded once at permanent defeat. Show remaining returns before battle; extra lives count in the encounter's difficulty budget. A dedicated undead Swarm needs weak bodies and its own strict limits rather than inheriting every faction mechanic automatically.

A boss could distribute its memories among visible anchors. Its exact recovery objective and phases would be authored separately. Friendly undead could include an archive keeper or a field medic whose own preserved body requires maintenance.

### Vampires

Retain recognizable blood-feeding and nocturnal themes. The setting can support ethical blood provision and medical professions as well as corrupted predation, without reducing every vampire to the same personality.

Prototype enemy sustain as healing from actual health damage inflicted on valid frontline targets. Fully absorbed hits provide no feeding, preserving shielding as a broadly reachable answer; breaks and focused damage are alternatives. Never bypass the safe back row just to express predation. Tune drain caps against downed-crew recovery so repeated revives cannot sustain a boss indefinitely.

An elite might protect a feeding partner; a boss could switch between a composed duelist and an exposed feeding phase. Neither should have universal control immunity or reset all player progress at a phase boundary.

## Broader possibilities, after these three

Orc warbands could emphasize coordinated charges and stagger; slimes could introduce bounded splitting and merging; fae could exchange buffs or distort targeting with clearly announced rules. Add them when their encounters create a distinct preparation problem. Do not expand the faction list faster than its members can receive recognizable artwork and working behavior.

Each faction should have a small initial kit: two regular enemies, one elite, one boss concept and one noncombat presence. Validate that shared faction rules do not combine with encounter objectives and voyage modifiers to demand an unavailable counter.

## Treasure carriers remain a separate role

A vault drone, hoarding beast, goblin runner or undead reliquary bearer can all serve as the voyage's “loot goblin.” They share visible wealth and a valuable death drop, while keeping the anatomy of their faction. Use a consistent treasure marker so their reward role is readable across different appearances.

Species, faction, combat format, elite status and treasure-carrier status remain distinct properties. See [loot goblins and Treasure caches](recovery-inbox-and-equipment-proposal.md#loot-goblins-and-treasure-caches) for rewards and the initial fleeing behavior proposal.
