# Visual identity — selected direction

## What the mark is

The selected primary mark is the formal circular university seal at
`assets/brand/star-singer-university-seal-v2.png`. This opaque-white raster is the visual source of
truth: navy whale-like hull, compact asymmetrical shell canopies, upright aft ring, broken gold
orbit, north star and lower crescent finial. The earlier transparent v1 remains a working study,
not the approved logo.

Vectorization is deliberately pending. The first SVG interpretation was rejected because it lost
too much of the ship's character; any future vector must be judged directly against v2.

The identifying features of *ARC 017 - Star Singer* are its calm, whale-like organic hull and the
enormous vertical spiral/ring structure at its aft. The mark should simplify the ship in side
profile rather than turn the ring into a generic infinity symbol.

At small sizes the mark needs only three pieces:

1. a long, low organic hull pointing forward;
2. one dominant open vertical ring at the aft;
3. a small star or aperture where hull and ring meet.

The full seal adds a broken gold orbit, north star and lower crescent finial. The lower hull uses two
shallow layers of three broad, short, asymmetrical shell skirts or armour canopies.
They are integrated hull architecture, not fins: they must never lengthen into biological whale
flippers or multiply into a feathered wing.

The ring sits behind the hull. Keeping that depth relationship consistent prevents the crossed
ribbons from reading as an infinity logo. A second fine inner ring can suggest resonance, but it
must disappear cleanly in the smallest version.

## Possible future system

- **Ship badge:** a reduced whale-vessel silhouette with the aft ring as its tail signature. Used
  on the hull, uniforms, launcher icon and anything specifically belonging to *Star Singer*.
- **Long Census mark:** a more abstract treatment of the same silhouette, potentially using the
  ring as a resonance aperture. Used for expedition equipment and catalogue entries.
- **University seal:** the approved ship or Census mark within a more formal seal. Used for degrees,
  faculty signage and formal documents.

The elemental stations use the established palette: Order cyan-blue, Chaos rose-crimson, Growth
green-teal, Decay sepia-ochre, Energy amber-gold and Void deep violet. The core badge must also work
in one colour, at 24 pixels, and as a stencil.

## Avoid

- a horizontally balanced infinity sign, which loses the ship silhouette;
- fine botanical detail inside the hull;
- literal musical notes;
- six equal colours in the everyday ship badge;
- text fused permanently to the icon.

The organization name and final wordmark remain open. The badge should not depend on either.
