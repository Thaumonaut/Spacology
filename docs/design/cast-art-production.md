# Cast art production

This is the authoritative art checklist for the playable cast. There are 37 playable characters: the original 28, Redcap, The Mage, Hanae, Arunima, Atsa, Ivara, Roonie, Veska and Daven. The six dedicated Aether Weavers are Hanae, Ivara, Arunima, Roonie, Veska and Daven. Silen is a separate portrait and lore concept, outside the playable roster. The three J3K robots are summons and are tracked separately below, not counted as playable characters.

The shared visual language is soft painterly anime/JRPG illustration, warm paper grain, an element-colored circular field, practical expedition clothing, and a pose that reveals personality. It is a rendering language, not a face template.

Status meanings: **approved** is the current selection; **candidate** is rendered and awaiting final approval; **queued** still needs a new portrait; **lore needed** has art but needs a final name or roster definition.

## Non-negotiable variety

- Do not reuse Stella's skin tone, curls, facial structure, glasses, or expression unless the character is Stella.
- Age, weight, height, musculature, disability, and conventional attractiveness vary independently.
- Fat characters are allowed to be elegant, athletic, flirtatious, awkward, scholarly, dangerous, and beautiful. They are not all tanks or comic relief.
- Large silhouettes include both muscular builds and soft builds: broad and buff, fat and strong, fat and soft, tall and heavy, and compact and stout.
- Moth-folk are very pale and visibly mothlike; Mana-born use blue, cyan, indigo, or other clearly nonhuman skin hues; Imke is human-forward with subtle fox traits.
- Fungal people use integrated mycelial anatomy rather than surface decoration.
- Culture, ancestry and species are independent axes. Prioritize memorable lore and species silhouettes first, then carry cultural identity through the person rather than quota-driven casting or costume shorthand.
- Vary camera angle, gaze, gesture, posture, and emotional register. Avoid a parade of sultry three-quarter faces looking right.
