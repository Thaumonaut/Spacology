Concept only; not a playable recruit yet. Current portrait: `assets/concepts/veiled-fungal-contemplative-v5.png`. The earlier portraits remain as visual explorations. Name, faith, homeworld and final kit remain open.

## Visual identity

Short, asymmetrical layers of warm blond-brown fungal hair have wavy gill-like locks and an irregular honeycombed crown woven into the top, following the stronger silhouette of `veiled-fungal-signal-analyst-v3.png`. A **single continuous white open-cell mushroom veil** falls from the crown, across her closed eyes, and down beside her cheeks and ears. Its thread thickness and cell size stay even across the eye region and the rest of the veil; there are no enlarged eye-shaped openings, visible eyelids, empty gaps, or separate solid blindfold. Her underlying eye line sits naturally lower on her face, with a short, proportionate nose. Pale skin, a gentle adult face and a small smile remain visible below the lace. The flowing ivory ceremonial mantle, long embroidered front panels, dark teal underlayer, pose and devotional beads follow `veiled-fungal-contemplative-v1.png`. She now holds a palm-sized crafted fungal devotional device with an ivory cap, bronze stem, and warm light between its gills. The living veil is part of her fungal anatomy and her chosen religious observance. It stays in place.

## Character direction

She is a respected contemplative and field scholar at the university. Her faith is an ordinary, accepted part of campus life, not a forbidden secret or proof of supernatural power. She wears the covering as a continuous practice of listening before judging by appearance. The veil's nonvisual sensitivity gives her a grounded way to navigate and do research. She is patient with people, quietly funny, and stubborn about testing her own interpretations. Her devotion shapes her values and habits; it does not make her a generic healer or oracle. The tradition's name and theology can be developed with the rest of the setting.

## Combat direction

**Job:** reactive sub-DPS, with a fixed-count bounce attack. The veil detects pressure and movement through the air, so enemy actions build a small reserve of signals. This makes a tank or barrier team an interesting partner: they can let an enemy act safely and convert that action into damage. A team that delays every enemy may give this character fewer signals. Her faith informs her patience and interpretation, while the combat mechanism comes from her sensory biology and equipment.

- **Basic — Read the Air:** light single-target damage and one Mark.
- **Skill — Lattice Return:** spend 2 Aether for three 80%-attack bounce hits. Visit unhit living enemies before repeating a target; repeated hits in the same cast deal half damage. Consume up to two stored signals for one additional bounce each. This provides useful damage without turning one boss into five full-strength hits.
- **Passive — Incoming Pattern:** gain one signal, up to two, after a scheduled enemy attack. Extra turns, ultimates and enemy recovery without an attack do not grant signals.
- **Ultimate — Vigil Pattern:** reveal the next enemy intent and make four 90%-attack bounces, following the same repeat-hit rule. The intent reveal needs a visible decision or preparation benefit before it becomes a real kit feature.

The numbers are starting hypotheses for simulation, not balance commitments. Compare damage per 100 AV, number of distinct targets hit, repeated-hit damage, enemy attacks endured, and whether a Hull partner changes the outcome. Test both multi-enemy waves and single bosses. Keep this character out of the active roster until the bounce targeting and visual feedback exist.
