SPACOLOGY / MINI-GAMES

Co-op station puzzles

ONE SHIP, THREE TEAMS

Exploration fights. Research and logistics make it possible.

Station puzzles for the two support teams. Research turns samples and signals into easier battles and blueprints; logistics builds, packs and moves the gear, supplies and samples. Night games prepare the next day; day games process what arrived. Each puzzle is generated from a seed and proven solvable. Rate each one after you finish: fun and annoyance are recorded separately.

Ratings, times and results are kept in this browser. “Export playtests” downloads them as JSON.
