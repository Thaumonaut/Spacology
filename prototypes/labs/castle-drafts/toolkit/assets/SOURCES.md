# Third-party assets

Kenney Nature Kit (2020), https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit
License: CC0 1.0, supplied license in KENNEY-LICENSE.txt.
Downloaded 2026-10-24 from the direct download linked on the author’s page.

Included models: tree_oak, plant_bushDetailed, rock_smallA, flower_purpleA.
Converted from OBJ/MTL to indexed triangles and diffuse colors in nature.json;
original geometry is preserved. Scene instances scale and tint these models.
No Unity packages or purchased assets are bundled.
