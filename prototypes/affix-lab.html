Every voyage rolls its conditions and bosses before you choose anyone. Recruit any crewmate you like: two start free, and each extra berth costs more than the last. The only randomness you buy is equipment: three offers per stop, rerolls rising. Six team types, each with its own sustainer. Nine fights, three sectors, and the first battles ramp up gently.

Experimental rules. A seeded model for the affix team-building design. Aether spending comes from the live aether-rules.js; the fights are a lab simulation, not the live resolver, and the kits are lab abstractions of the real characters. Progress stays in this browser.
