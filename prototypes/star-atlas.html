Birthplace and ancestry

Birthplace records where someone grew up. Ancestral-world affiliation records a shared lineage. A shipborn moth-person can share Tessine with someone raised on its surface. A human from Tannhul may share Earth ancestry without losing their Tannhul identity.

One world, several peoples

Calyx brings fungal people and oni together. Ilex connects cervine and ursine crew. Worlds also contain migrant communities: species is a starting point for these proposed affiliations, not an exclusive population rule.

2 crew: +8% base health to the team. 3 crew: +15% instead. Different worlds add together. Only deployed crew count.

Existing world names and recorded birthplaces come from the design notes. Star placements, some ecologies, ancestral assignments and the harmony values are proposals for testing. Read the mapping proposal.
